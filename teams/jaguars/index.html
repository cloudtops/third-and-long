2026 · Preseason

South #2 · 2025 13-4 · Breakout: RB Bhayshul Tuten

The Jaguars, one of the NFL’s most improved teams last year, are coming in hot off the tail of an AFC South division title and a 13-win season. After losing a heartbreaker to Buffalo in the playoffs, Jacksonville looks to replicate last year’s regular season success with a more balanced offensive attack (Enter RB Chris Rodriguez Jr to complement young emergent talent RB Bhayshul Tuten), and finally look for QB Trevor Lawrence to play at an elite level for an entire season. Lawrence was great at times last year, but for Jacksonville to finally get over the hump they need an MVP-caliber season from the Clemson product.

2025 · Midseason

Ranked #18 · 5-4

Jacksonville started the year off burning hot, riding a historically opportunistic defense to victories by capitalizing off turnovers. Things have slowed down in a big way lately, and the biggest culprit behind the team’s lack of success is QB Trevor Lawrence. Lawrence has never grown into the elite quarterback that was promised to Jags fans 5 years ago, and his performance throughout his career is holding the team back. Regardless of how hot the defense was at the beginning of the year, that style of winning football games was never going to be sustainable, and the offense needed to explode in terms of productivity to give fans any semblance of stability for the rest of the year. That production has been nowhere to be seen, and the Jags look like a team that will finish around .500 and miss the postseason.

2025 · Preseason

South #2 · 2024 4-13 · Breakout: WR/CB Travis Hunter (R)

Jacksonville is a tricky team to nail down for 2025. The Jaguars success this year will rely on how big of a step QB Trevor Lawrence takes in his fifth season, as the highly touted first-overall pick in 2021 hasn’t done anything to propel the Jags to any sort of postseason prosperity. He will need to have a huge year, relying on superstar WR Brian Thomas Jr. in order to have any sort of chance at turning things around in Duval. New HC Liam Coen, who masterminded Tampa Bay’s offensive prowess over the last two seasons, has his hands full with a fairly mediocre roster and will need to really lock in for the Jags to have any sort of success.

2024 · Midseason

Ranked #27 · 2-7

The Jacksonville Jaguars have been one of the league's most disappointing clubs this season. Hopes were high to start things off this year, and the team believed this was the year former first overall pick Trevor Lawrence made the jump from average to elite. That has not been the case. Lawrence has not been anything special this season, and although he has been consistently let down by his receiving corps (the Jags lead the league in dropped passes through the last three seasons), their quarterback has not been good enough to lead this team to the playoffs. This is even considering the emergence of rookie LSU receiver Brian Thomas Jr., who leads all rookies in receiving yards and continues the trend of LSU receivers just being better than everyone else on the field. The end of the Doug Pederson era seems imminent in Duval, and although the team has looked scrappier over the last few weeks since their 0-5 start, the Jags retain their place among the NFL's bottom feeders.
