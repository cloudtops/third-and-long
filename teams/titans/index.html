2026 · Preseason

South #4 · 2025 3-14 · Breakout: WR Wan’Dale Robinson

I have a lot of hope for the Titans this season, I just think the AFC South is going to be too competitive in 2026 for a team that is still clearly rebuilding to throw any sort of wrench into things. Second year QB Cam Ward will take a massive leap forward due to the additions of first-round WR Carnell Tate, who dazzled at Ohio State last season, and WR Wan’Dale Robinson, who is sneaky underrated and will be a PPR monster for fantasy owners this year as Tate acclimatizes to the speed of the pro game. Tennessee’s defense was surprisingly stout over the first half of the year before regressing to finish with a 28th-overall ranking. DT Jeffrey Simmons is still an elite playmaker and will anchor a defense that will likely improve somewhat.

2025 · Midseason

Ranked #32 · 1-8

A tough start to the beginning of QB Cam Ward’s career in 2025. The #1 overall pick has shown a number of flashes this season, but without any sort of support around him, the fact that they have a single win this year is probably something good to build on. The Titans are inept organizationally, and are awful on both sides of the ball. Ward is relatively innocent, as a terrible defense that can’t stop a nose bleed and a geriatric supporting cast on offense do not make a strong developmental foundation for a young quarterback. There are basically no positives this season for Tennessee, and they will likely finish the year 1-16 with the #1 overall pick in next year’s draft.

2025 · Preseason

South #3 · 2024 3-14 · Breakout: QB Cam Ward (R)

Last year’s bottom dwellers, the Titans believe they have struck gold with first overall pick QB Cam Ward. HC Brian Callahan enters year two of Tennessee’s rebuild with a shiny new toy under center, and although Ward will undoubtedly run into some growing pains, especially early on against some tough defenses, the Titans should be able to improve significantly after the dumpster fire that was 2024. The Titans’ defensive unit was actually pretty good last year, and with the offensive additions of WR Tyler Lockett and WR Van Jefferson, Ward will have a solid veteran receiving corps to rely on.

2024 · Midseason

Ranked #29 · 2-6

We now move in to the tier of teams where there are some legitimate positives to speak about! Very exciting. The Titans are currently the owners of the NFL's best defense in total yards against. This unit has quietly excelled this season, and like Las Vegas before them, any sort of offensive production would undoubtedly put this squad towards the middle of the pack. Things have been disastrous offensively, however. Second year Kentucky product Will Levis has been atrocious this season, and Mason Rudolph hasn't been anything special in relief of Levis' recent shoulder sprain. The Titans are another team that is severely lacking in the playmaker department. Tony Pollard was shipped to Tennessee from Dallas in the offseason and has been underwhelming in his role as the Titans' leading back. Expect the Titans to grind out a few more wins this season in a poor division on the back of their surprisingly stingy defense.
