2026 · Preseason

West #2 · 2025 14-3 · Breakout: RB Jadarian Price (R)

Wildly underrated as I see it entering the 2026 season, it feels like nobody is really talking about Seattle for a team that firmly cemented themselves as the NFL’s best team by winning the Super Bowl last season. Everyone is talking about the Rams and their ridiculous additions, and rightfully so, but the Seahawks seem to strangely be flying under the radar for a team of their calibre. QB Sam Darnold led this group to the promised land in 2025 behind a ridiculously efficient and turnover-avoidant scheme, with help from 2025’s OPOTY WR Jaxon Smith-Njigba. WR Rashid Shaheed seems to be making large gains in the offseason as well and should fit in as a more significant part of the offense in 2026. The departure of Super Bowl MVP Kenneth Walker for Kansas City certainly hurts, but the one-two punch of RB Zach Charbonnet (when he returns from injury) and rookie RB Jadarian Price should give Seattle a strong and balanced offense. The defense is elite, led by CB Devon Witherspoon, DT Leonard Williams, and emergent breakout-star candidate DT Byron Murphy. Seattle should absolutely be in contention for a repeat title this year, as it appears the road to glory runs through the best division in football.

2025 · Midseason

Ranked #1 · 7-2

Seattle is the league’s most complete team through ten weeks, and it hasn’t been particularly close. In a season with questions abound for some of the perennial top contenders, the Seahawks have risen significantly above middling preseason expectations to claim the midseason ranking’s top spot. QB Sam Darnold has been on fire in his new home, boasting the league’s highest QBR and showing strong command of the offense all while protecting the football. Young WR Jaxon Smith-Njigba is a legitimate threat to break Calvin Johnson’s all-time receiving yards record, and has been simply unstoppable this season. The defense is all world, and has absolutely stifled opposing offenses all season, even with a few injuries throughout the course of the year. It will be an exciting finish in the league’s best division to end the year, but Seattle looks like a true contender.

2025 · Preseason

West #1 · 2024 10-7 · Breakout: TE Elijah Arroyo (R)

Okay, okay, hear me out. Last season, QB Sam Darnold relied on a strong defense and a superstar wide receiver to guide the Minnesota Vikings to a 14 win season. What does Darnold inherit in Seattle? An excellent young defense, and an emerging superstar in WR Jaxon Smith-Njigba. Second year HC Mike Macdonald took a ragtag group to the brink of playoff contention last year, and after overachieving last year with QB Geno Smith, Seattle will ride the more conservative play style of Darnold to their first division title in years. The strength of this team is certainly their defensive unit, but their offense will surprise people this season.

2024 · Midseason

Ranked #17 · 4-5

Seattle has been a bit of an enigma this season. Following a 3-0 start, the Seahawks have regressed on both sides of the ball. Veteran journeyman QB Geno Smith is one of the league's true gunslingers, and his boom-or-bust play style has both won and lost games for Seattle this season. Superstar WR DK Metcalf's absence has also been a significant blow to the offense, as a knee sprain has limited their offense's ability to crack big plays, as Metcalf was among the league's premier deep threats before his injury. After a brilliant start from the defensive unit, injuries have severely impacted their ability to make stops, although rookie DT Byron Murphy Jr. has shown up for Seattle as one of the league's premier young interior defensive linemen. Second year corner Devon Witherspoon continues to be one of the most exciting defensive players to watch in the NFC, and expect one of the league's youngest teams to evolve into a legitimate threat in the next few seasons.
