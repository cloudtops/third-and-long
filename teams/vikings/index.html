2026 · Preseason

North #4 · 2025 9-8 · Breakout: QB Kyler Murray

It feels like a decade ago when the Vikings won 14 games and made the playoffs. It was actually only two years back, but last year’s middling 9-8 campaign led to a lot of people souring on the Purple People Eaters. The experiment with drafting a raw rookie quarterback in JJ McCarthy has been a pretty abject failure, and Minnesota has now turned to the services of veteran QB Kyler Murray to right the ship and get the offense moving back in the right direction. WR Justin Jefferson is still one of the game’s elite playmaking receivers, and TE TJ Hockenson should be able to bail Murray out of some 3rd-and-long situations. Minnesota does actually have one of the league’s best defenses (7th in scoring last season), but the success of this team will certainly depend on whether Murray is able to replicate the magic of his rookie season seven years later.

2025 · Midseason

Ranked #22 · 4-5

The Vikings have taken an expected step back this season behind the helm of QB JJ McCarthy. McCarthy, who was Minnesota’s first round pick last year, did not play in 2024, so significant growing pains were to be expected. There is reason to be hopeful in Minnesota, as fans can likely count on HC Kevin O’Connell, one of the league’s best, to be able to mold McCarthy into a winning QB. The defense has been middling, and key departures on that side of the ball have made the Vikings too reliant on heroics from what is essentially a rookie quarterback. More troubling, All Pro WR Justin Jefferson has had his least productive season yet and has been a virtual no-show in games where McCarthy starts. It will be a few years before Minnesota finds themselves relevant again, and that is contingent on the development of their second-year signal caller.

2025 · Preseason

North #4 · 2024 14-3 · Breakout: QB JJ McCarthy

The team with the strongest regression in 2025 will be the Minnesota Vikings, who shocked the league last season by winning 14 games behind their strong defense and strong play of QB Sam Darnold. Darnold earned himself a nice payday out on the West Coast this offseason, so it is now up to second year QB JJ McCarthy to guide the Vikings through this rebuilding period. McCarthy is essentially a rookie, after missing all of 2024 with injury, and Minnesota will experience legitimate growing pains in the league’s toughest division en route to missing the playoffs following a 14 win season.

2024 · Midseason

Ranked #7 · 6-2

Minnesota started the season out as the league's lone undefeated squad, firing on all cylinders at 5-0 through five weeks. The last three weeks have been tough for Minnesota, after dropping two straight to Detroit and Los Angeles, they rebounded last week, albeit narrowly, against Indianapolis. QB Sam Darnold has been having a career renaissance this season, and that success can likely be chalked up to the presence of WR Justin Jefferson. Once again, we are spouting praise for another LSU receiver, but over the last few seasons, Jefferson has earned every single cent of the contract that the Vikings have given him. He rivals only his former teammate Ja'Marr Chase in explosivity. If the Vikings can continue their offensive production, and Defensive Coordinator Brian Flores' elite game planning, the Vikings can challenge for a top four NFC playoff seed and a deep playoff run.
