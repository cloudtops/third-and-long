2026 · Preseason

West #1 · 2025 14-3 · Breakout: WR Pat Bryant

It is certainly no steaming hot take to ascertain that, barring the injury to QB Bo Nix at the end of a divisional playoff game last year, Denver almost would’ve certainly beat New England in the AFC Championship and advanced to face Seattle in the Super Bowl. However, that was then, and this is now, and Nix looks to be fully healthy and ready to shoulder the burden of being one of the league’s most talked about Super Bowl favorites. Denver’s offense was good enough last year, but the addition of WR Jaylen Waddle gives the unit a tremendous boost in firepower. The offensive line remains one of the best in football. The defense is the heart and soul of this team, however, as DE Nik Bonitto and former DPOTY CB Pat Surtain II anchor what was the second-overall ranked defense in football last season. Denver should be able to get over the hump and compete for a Lombardi Trophy in 2026.

2025 · Midseason

Ranked #12 · 8-2

Who are the Denver Broncos? That is a question that will certainly be answered in the coming weeks, as Sean Payton’s team looks to ride one of the league’s best defenses into postseason glory. There are no questions on that side of the ball, as EDGE Nik Bonitto and CB Patrick Surtain II are two of the most impactful players in the entire NFL, and the entire unit is about as staunch as it gets against both the run and the pass. The questions for Denver lie on offense, as second year QB Bo Nix has taken a pretty noticeable step back this season. He looks unsure of himself and more reluctant to use his athleticism to make plays, often holding the ball for too long and forcing Denver into difficult conversion scenarios. If Nix can right the ship, Denver should be contending deep into the postseason.

2024 · Midseason

Ranked #16 · 5-4

The Broncos are another club that bet on a young quarterback early in the draft, and this gamble has appeared to be paying off for them thus far. Oregon QB Bo Nix has had his up and downs through nine weeks, but has recently been showing why Denver took the chance on him by limiting turnovers and showing legitimate toughness running the football. Veteran WR Courtland Sutton is developing a strong chemistry with Nix, and they have recently been doing a much better job of running the football. The real story in Denver lies on the other side of the ball, however. The Broncos' defense has been elite so far this year, and CB Patrick Surtain II is a big reason why this team is off to a surprising start, and has a chance for a berth in the AFC playoffs. Denver's defense is currently holding teams to 17.9 points/game, good for third in the league, and this is the main reason they find themselves in the top half of the rankings.
