2026 · Preseason

South #4 · 2025 8-9 · Breakout: DT Brandon Dorlus

Atlanta has a decent roster on paper. A lot of firepower on offense (RB Bijan Robinson, WR Drake London, TE Kyle Pitts), and a defense that looks to be improving a lot over last year with the additions of LBs Azeez Ojulari and Za’Darius Smith. CB AJ Terrell is one of the best cover corners in football, and S Jessie Bates III is a potential All Pro at his position as well. The main problem in Atlanta is the most important part of having a successful football team, which is their Quarterbacking Conundrum. After the shocking first round selection of Washington standout QB Michael Penix Jr a few years back, things haven’t really panned out for the southpaw. The addition of QB Tua Tagovailoa from Miami further muddies the waters for Atlanta. For this team to have any sort of success in 2026, they need to firmly establish their offensive identity, and fast.

2025 · Midseason

Ranked #19 · 3-6

Disappointing, to say the least for Falcons fans. In what was supposed to be a breakout year for QB Michael Penix Jr, the team has yet to find an identity on offense. This team is much better than their record states, but 3-6 is 3-6. The offense should be extremely explosive on paper, but they haven’t been able to string success along for longer than four quarters. RB Bijan Robinson is probably the most electric player playing on Sundays, but the play calling and lack of offensive rhythm is not doing him justice and severely knee-capping his potential. The defense has surprisingly been elite, with the fourth best EPA against so far this season, but the team has been unable to play complementary football and the Win-Loss column reflects that.

2025 · Preseason

South #3 · 2024 8-9 · Breakout: QB Michael Penix Jr.

Atlanta is a bit of an enigma for me this season. Their success hinges on the emergence of their second year QB Michael Penix Jr., who played fairly well in only three appearances last season. The jury is out on Penix, but he does have a strong offensive line and the ability to rely on fantastic offensive weapons WR Drake London (barring injury) and RB Bijan Robinson. The offense should be good, but the defense is what will drag the team down in 2024. Playing a relatively weak schedule will help Atlanta, but the up-and-down play of unproven Michael Penix Jr. will have the Dirty Birds missing out on the postseason yet again.

2024 · Midseason

Ranked #11 · 6-3

Atlanta has had a surprising start to the 2024 season. After signing veteran quarterback Kirk Cousins from Minnesota, the Falcons have finally utilized their recent first round draft picks on offense to turn into one of the league's premier scoring threats. RB Bijan Robinson and TE Kyle Pitts are having breakout seasons, and the addition of Chicago WR Darnell Mooney is allowing Cousins to flourish in the A. Cousins has been balling out, as evidenced by a league-high 509 yard passing performance earlier this year, and his offensive stewardship has Atlanta in the driver's seat in the NFC South. The defense, which has been suspect in past seasons, has been gelling this year behind the solid play of former All Pro cornerback AJ Terrell. Atlanta has been one of the league's best surprises this season.
