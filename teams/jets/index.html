2025 · Midseason

Ranked #31 · 2-7

Too many expectations in year one for new HC Aaron Glenn. The Jets are coming off back to back wins after starting the season 0-7, but the problems that have plagued the franchise for years now have not been assuaged by Glenn’s arrival. QB Justin Fields has been terrible, but with the nagging injuries to WR Garrett Wilson and RB Breece Hall, the blame does not fall entirely on him. The biggest story with the Jets is that they have officially entered total shut down mode after dealing CB Sauce Gardner and DT Quinnen Williams at the deadline, arguably the team’s two best players. We will see if the move for hoarding draft picks pays off for the Jets, but for now, they remain in the basement of the NFL.

2024 · Midseason

Ranked #23 · 3-6

The Jets have been, in my opinion, the league's most disappointing team so far this season. The return of four time MVP Aaron Rodgers following an achilles tear on the fourth play of the season last year, coupled with an elite defense had the Jets faithful hopeful for a deep playoff run. Things have gone the opposite way so far this year. Losses have just kept piling up for New York following a 2-1 start, and Rodgers appears to be showing his age as the league's oldest player. A marquee win against Houston in primetime last week has some folks believing in the Jets again, but if the offense continues to look as inept as it has so far this year, the Jets will have a top 10 pick in the draft come April. It's not all bad in the Big Apple, though, as second year receiver Garrett Wilson is emerging into a legitimate superstar and Rodgers' favorite target, which is a testament to his skill as Rodgers' former teammates Allen Lazard and Davante Adams are catching passes for New York as well. The Jets could make things interesting down the stretch if the defense continues to be elite and Rodgers can continue to gel with his guys.
