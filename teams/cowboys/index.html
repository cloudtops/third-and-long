2026 · Preseason

East #2 · 2025 7-9-1 · Breakout: S Caleb Downs (R)

Look at me, Cowboys fans. Look me in the eye. It’s not your year. The Dallas Cowboys are an enigma. The offense looks primed for another ridiculously successful campaign, boasting a strong running game (RB Javonte Williams), an elite offensive line, one of the best WR corps in the league (CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens), and consistent quarterback play from QB Dak Prescott. The problem with Dallas is that they have the worst defense in football. No amount of moves from owner Jerry Jones last year could salvage what was the worst statistical unit in football. Rookie S Caleb Downs could give Dallas’ defense the adrenaline shot they need to compete, but my prediction is that Dallas averages 35+ points per game and still misses the playoffs.

2025 · Midseason

Ranked #20 · 3-4-1

If the offense isn’t breaking records, this team simply has no chance to win. With defensive statistics trailing only the free falling Cincinnati Bengals, there is very little hope that Dallas has any chance to make the postseason this year or in the future. The old adage holds true, and defense not only wins championships but also wins games at this level (see: Denver Broncos), and with the offense falling back to earth in recent weeks, things are looking bleak in Jerryworld. The offseason gamble to deal all-world EDGE Micah Parsons to Green Bay has resulted in the second-worst defense in pro football, and GM/Owner Jerry Jones is finally looking like he is in over his head after a legendary tenure helming America’s Team. QB Dak Prescott has been fantastic this season, pairing especially well with the addition of WR George Pickens. RB Javonte Williams has been excellent as well, but the Cowboys stand no chance without a defense.

2025 · Preseason

East #3 · 2024 7-10 · Breakout: WR George Pickens

“It’s our year! It’s our year!” Cowboys fans chant in unison after another season of not winning the Super Bowl. Dallas had an uncharacteristic regular season last year, not even waiting until January to break their fans’ hearts. It should be a better year record-wise for Dallas this season, although offseason turbulence has not been pretty so far for Jerry and Co. All-Pro LB Micah Parsons is still holding out, and the situation between him and Jerry Jones seems irreparable. The offense should look better after adding Steelers WR George Pickens, as this will likely stop teams from double-teaming star WR CeeDee Lamb every down. However, Dallas has once again refused to address their abysmal rushing offense, as well as rushing defense, and this oversight will once again have America’s Team on their couches come playoff time.

2024 · Midseason

Ranked #22 · 3-5

I'd like to state my bias early in this one… nothing has made me happier this season than the implosion of the Dallas Cowboys. The departure of running back Tony Pollard has crippled the offense, as the Cowboys' offense has been the definition of a one-trick pony, asking All Pro receiver CeeDee Lamb to carry the team on his back. Lamb has again been elite this year, but QB Dak Prescott has been seeing ghosts this year and continues to make head-scratching play after head-scratching play as the offense continues to sputter. The defense has also been alarmingly mediocre this season, as the absence of superstar linebacker Micah Parsons has severely crippled this unit and the Cowboys look to be in freefall over the last few weeks, losing their last three contests. Expect Head Coach Mike McCarthy to get the axe if things continue to go the way they've been going in JerryWorld.
