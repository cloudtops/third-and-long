2026 · Preseason

West #4 · 2025 3-14 · Breakout: LB Nakobe Dean

After the dismal season that was 2025, the Raiders should actually have a lot to be excited about entering 2026. After spending their first overall draft pick on reigning Heisman winner QB Fernando Mendoza, who led a scrappy Indiana Hoosier team to their first-ever national championship, Raiders fans are very intrigued to see what the future holds. Although Mendoza will likely ride pine behind QB Kirk Cousins (which is, in my opinion, the best thing a team can do for a young, promising quarterback), Vegas’ offense actually looks kind of dangerous with TE Brock Bowers and RB Ashton Jeanty looking to come into their own in their third and second seasons respectively. The defense added LB Kwity Paye and LB Nakobe Dean in the offseason, who will help this young unit gel alongside elite pass rusher DE Maxx Crosby.

2025 · Midseason

Ranked #28 · 2-7

The offseason dice roll that the Raiders made for veteran QB Geno Smith has not paid off. Smith has been one of the league’s worst quarterbacks, and the success of the team has followed suit. Not much to be said for Vegas outside of the encouraging rookie year of RB Ashton Jeanty and the continued dominance of EDGE Maxx Crosby. Jeanty has been effective, but a rookie running back cannot solely provide the spark needed to consistently win NFL football games. TE Brock Bowers is still elite, but his season has been limited with injuries. Until they invest a high draft pick in an elite quarterback prospect, the Raiders will continue to be the bottom-feeders of the meat grinder that is the AFC West.

2025 · Preseason

West #4 · 2024 4-13 · Breakout: RB Ashton Jeanty (R)

Las Vegas is brand new on offense this season, and the defense also looks much improved. Vegas’ front office made the correct decision in paying superstar DE Maxx Crosby, and has also bolstered the unit by adding a plethora of veteran defensive backs and linebackers. The most exciting piece of this team this season will be the emergence of rookie RB Ashton Jeanty. Jeanty had one of the most dominant college seasons by a running back in recent memory last year at Boise State and had a very strong end to the preseason. Newly acquired QB Geno Smith should be able to get cooking quickly with star TE Brock Bowers, and the Raiders should significantly improve to round out one of the league’s strongest divisions.

2024 · Midseason

Ranked #30 · 2-7

Not many positives to speak on so far through 9 weeks of Raider football. Their league-average defense has been the team's calling card this season, but the blame for their 2-7 record ultimately falls to poor coaching and execution on the offensive side of the ball. Washington State legend Gardner Minshew has been inconsistent, and the Raiders are desperate for a playmaker for him to get the ball to. The losses of Josh Jacobs and Davante Adams have crippled this offense, although rookie tight end Brock Bowers currently leads all tight ends in receiving yards and looks like a future perennial Pro Bowler. Defensive Player of the Year candidate Maxx Crosby is essentially carrying this team on his back right now, but it has not been enough. Another lost season for the hapless Raiders.
