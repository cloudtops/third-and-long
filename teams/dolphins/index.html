2025 · Midseason

Ranked #25 · 3-7

An absolutely pitiful start to the season has been unsuccessfully mitigated by the Dolphins, continuing to play losing football even with the increased quality of play from QB Tua Tagovailoa. HC Mike McDaniel’s tenure appears to be over, despite a confounding, dominant win over the Bills recently. The departure of key defensive fixtures have identified the Dolphins as sellers moving into the offseason, and it appears as a full overhaul is in the works for Miami. Lone bright spot RB De’Von Achane has been terrific this season, and could be an attractive trade target if the Dolphins do decide to clean house. That 70-30 win over the Broncos a few years ago seems further away than the Fins’ last Super Bowl appearance (1985).

2025 · Preseason

East #3 · 2024 8-9 · Breakout: DE Chop Robinson

Miami has fallen from grace in recent years, going from one of the NFL’s most exciting teams to one of the league’s most middling. Constantly plagued by the injury bug, it will be up to QB Tua Tagovailoa to guide the fast-paced offense back to contention against an average schedule this season. WRs Tyreek Hill and Jaylen Waddle will need to bounce back from mediocre campaigns last year if Miami has any chance of making it to the postseason. The defensive side of the ball has also been lambasted by injuries recently, but if DE Jaelan Phillips can stay healthy and complement future breakout superstar DE Chop Robinson, the Dolphins may be able to turn things around.

2024 · Midseason

Ranked #24 · 2-7

A very disappointing start to the season for Mike McDaniel's ball club. Following yet another scary concussion to QB Tua Tagovailoa, the Dolphins were essentially dead in the water and looked to be one of the league's worst offenses, with the stable of backup QBs Snoop Huntley, Skylar Thompson, and Tim Boyle all failing to effectively utilize all-world receiver Tyreek Hill. Significant injuries to the defense have really kneecapped them as well, and Miami looked like they were essentially finished for the year. Enter Tua. After losing a close game to Arizona in his return, the Dolphins went toe to toe with the Bills last week and only lost on a 61-yard field goal from Buffalo kicker Tyler Bass. Miami might not be in a position to make a playoff push, but with a wide open wild card picture in the AFC, anything is possible. After all, this is the same team that scored 70 points in a single game last season.
