2026 · Preseason

East #1 · 2025 12-5 · Breakout: DT Deone Walker

A bounce back season for the Bills seems to be in order. After another absolute postseason heartbreaker at the hands of Sean Payton, Buffalo has finally made a significant change by ousting HC Sean McDermott (one of the winningest bosses in franchise history), and replacing him with former offensive coordinator Joe Brady. The offense has added playmaking WR DJ Moore from Chicago, and Buffalo boasts one of the best QB/RB duos in the league with QB Josh Allen and RB James Cook. The defense remains largely the same, (although the introduction of a 3-4 scheme to the unit should shake things up somewhat) but the addition of DE Bradley Chubb should help out DE Gregory Rousseau, and a pass rush that was ranked 27th in PRWR in 2025.

2025 · Midseason

Ranked #8 · 6-3

What a strange season in Buffalo. It has been hard to get a read on the league’s top contenders from year’s past this season, and Buffalo remains one of the most apparent enigmas through the halfway point. They beat the Chiefs in a thriller one week, then get decimated the next by an atrocious Miami team. QB Josh Allen is still fantastic, but the lack of support around him is starting to catch up, and Allen can’t do it all by himself every week. The defense has also taken a significant step back which puts even more pressure on the offense. It will be up to HC Sean McDermott to guide his team over the hump and past the rival Chiefs come January, or else Bills fans will likely be asking for some sort of large-scale structural change.

2025 · Preseason

East #1 · 2024 13-4 · Breakout: WR Keon Coleman

Another heartbreaking AFC Championship for Buffalo last year, as MVP QB Josh Allen and the Bills were unable to beat the Chiefs on the AFC’s biggest stage once again. In what was supposed to be a “rebuild, reload” year last year, Buffalo dominated throughout the regular season and rode Allen’s MVP season to a deep playoff run. This season, the Bills look primed and ready for another go at the Lombardi, and this will be the year they finally get over the hump and make it to the big game. The AFC East will largely be a cakewalk for the squad this season, and Bills Mafia will be able to defeat Mahomes and Co. in order to advance to their fifth Super Bowl appearance.

2024 · Midseason

Ranked #4 · 7-2

Another routine season for Buffalo. The Bills basically had their playoff ticket punched in week 6, and that privilege not only was allowed due to the stink of the rest of their division, but because their QB Josh Allen continues to play at an All-World level. Allen is a dark horse favorite for MVP, and after early season stumbles, the Bills look like their old selves again. The defense has looked pretty mediocre, and that only stands as a testament to how well Allen has been playing through nine weeks. The addition of former Pro Bowler Amari Cooper only helps Buffalo's passing attack following the departures of WR Stefon Diggs and WR Gabe Davis. The only thing that stands in the way of a Super Bowl berth is, wait for it Buffalo fans, Arrowhead Stadium.
