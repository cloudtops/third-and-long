2026 · Preseason

West #3 · 2025 12-5 · Breakout: WR De’Zhaun Stribling (R)

For whatever reason, I just don’t really believe in the 49ers this season. HC Kyle Shanahan is a great leader, and this team should absolutely be competitive, but I think their division and conference as a whole is just too difficult to really make any noise come playoff time. QB Brock Purdy returns to lead the offense, coupled with All Pro RB Christian McCaffrey and will lean on the new-look receiving corps of WR Mike Evans and rookie WR De’Zhaun Stribling. The defense gets a big boost with the arrival of new defensive coordinator Raheem Morris, and the return of LB Fred Warner across LB Dre Greenlaw should see this unit towards the top half of overall rankings come season’s end. There’s a lot to be excited about in the Bay Area, but the uphill battle is just too steep to climb this year.

2025 · Midseason

Ranked #15 · 6-3

Tough to get a read on the Niners this year. Yet again, San Francisco has been bitten hard by the injury bug, and it warrants the conversation at this point if the team needs a significant overhaul in terms of the training staff department. Backup QB Mac Jones has filled in admirably this year for Brock Purdy, but at this point, the injuries feel like too much to overcome. The loss of LB Fred Warner really cripples the spirit of this team, and although RB Christian McCaffrey has been playing at an elite level this year, there is simply too much competition within the division and the conference to predict a playoff appearance for San Francisco.

2025 · Preseason

West #3 · 2024 6-11 · Breakout: WR Ricky Pearsall

I just don’t believe in San Francisco going into 2025. Although they have one of the easiest schedule in the league this year, the team did not do enough to mitigate their injury issues last year to show me that their Super Bowl window hasn’t slammed shut. Departures of S Talanoa Hufanga and WR Deebo Samuel will hurt the team, and although RB Christian McCaffrey returns to the lineup, it won’t be enough to address the lingering injury concerns with most of the roster heading into this season. QB Brock Purdy is not enough of a game breaker to put the team on his back, and San Francisco will follow 2024’s disappointment with another year of missing the postseason.

2024 · Midseason

Ranked #18 · 4-4

A team that should climb on this list as the season continues, the 49ers have gone from a top three team in the league last season to an average- to- mediocre squad. An injury-riddled roster and regression from their young quarterback Brock Purdy are mostly to blame for their sluggish start after a loss in the Super Bowl last season. Reigning Offensive Player of the Year Christian McCaffrey has still yet to take a snap in San Francisco's backfield, and although RB Jordan Mason has been carrying the torch well in CMC's absence, the team seems to be missing the spark that has propelled them deep into the postseason recently. The defense remains a top 10 unit, and the 49ers should continue to move up this list if Shanahan can figure things out offensively.
