2026 · Preseason

South #1 · 2025 8-9 · Breakout: DE Reuben Bain Jr (R)

Tampa Bay rode the hot hand of QB Baker Mayfield to a blazing start out of the gate last season before crashing back to earth over the second half of the year in a campaign that saw them lose eight of their last eleven games. This team should certainly be better than last year, as a lot of the disaster that was the second half of 2025 can be attributed to injuries. Mayfield will lean on the return of WR Chris Godwin as well as the continued emergence of second year WR Emeka Egbuka. The defense should be top 10 this season, led by elite players S Antoine Winfield Jr and DT Vita Vea, as well as the addition of first round pick DE Reuben Bain Jr. Tampa Bay should win this division handily if all goes according to plan.

2025 · Midseason

Ranked #10 · 6-3

The early season darling Tampa Bay Buccaneers are another potential contender who have cooled off in recent weeks. QB Baker Mayfield began the year playing at an MVP-caliber level, but has regressed slightly from his early season highs, although he has still been playing well with a significantly depleted cast around him. Plenty is still going right down south, and the Bucs have a relatively straightforward path to their sixth-straight division title and a playoff berth. This is still a complete football team, and if Mayfield can get back to playing his hard-nosed brand of football and limiting the turnovers, Tampa Bay will be a force to be reckoned with in the postseason. Rookie WR Emeka Egbuka looks like the clear frontrunner for the Offensive Rookie of the Year Award, the latest in a long line of Ohio State products who have turned heads at the pro level immediately. Mayfield and his Bucs will cruise to the division title by season’s end.

2025 · Preseason

South #1 · 2024 10-7 · Breakout: WR Emeka Egbuka (R)

Tampa Bay surprised a lot of people last season, boasting one of the league’s most explosive offenses paired with a strong defensive unit. But they didn’t surprise me. QB Baker Mayfield put together an MVP-caliber campaign last season, and he has finally blossomed into one of the league’s best signal callers. Their offense boasts future Hall of Fame WR Mike Evans, WR Chris Godwin, who is coming off of a down 2024 but should bounce back this year, second year RB Bucky Irving, who took the league by storm last season, and rookie WR Emeka Egbuka, who the Bucs took in the top ten of the draft and should provide Mayfield with another strong target. Tampa Bay continues their NFC South dominance in 2025 en route to their fifth straight division title.

2024 · Midseason

Ranked #13 · 4-5

The story of Tampa Bay this season has been the continued excellence of 6th-year signal caller Baker Mayfield. Mayfield has once again defied the odds and has Tampa Bay's offense firing on all cylinders, even without his two best receivers in future Hall of Famer Mike Evans and veteran Chris Godwin. Mayfield has lit up virtually every defense he has faced up to this point, although he does need to limit his takeaways moving forward. A key facet of the Bucs' success on offense this season has been their surprising ability to run the football. Rookie RB Bucky Irving has been shining so far this season behind Tampa Bay's excellent offensive line, spearheaded by superstar LT Tristan Wirfs. Wirfs has quietly been one of the NFL's best players and is a key reason why Tampa Bay is in the mix for the NFC South title. They may not have a winning record at this point, but their ability to take Kansas City to the wire in primetime last week shows this is a legit squad that could make noise in a stacked NFC.
