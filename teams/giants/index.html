2026 · Preseason

East #3 · 2025 4-13 · Breakout: LB Arvell Reese (R)

The New York Giants may actually be competitive in 2026 following a lot of shakeups in the Big Apple, most notably the addition of HC John Harbaugh. Harbaugh will bring a hard-nosed attitude to a team that has done a great job building their roster through the draft over the last few years, and this year under Harbaugh’s leadership they may surprise a lot of people. The electric offensive combination of second-year players QB Jaxson Dart and RB Cam Skattebo will continue to hit their stride, with help from the return of elite WR Malik Nabers, whose 2025 campaign was cut short prematurely due to injury. On the other side of the ball, the defensive front seven is legitimately terrifying. Brian Burns, Kayvon Thibodeaux, Abdul Carter, and highly touted Arvell Reese make up a pass-rushing unit that is, on paper, probably the most dangerous in football. If Harbaugh can give this team an identity, the Giants should experience more success than expected in 2026.

2025 · Midseason

Ranked #29 · 2-8

Hope has arrived in New Jersey! Although, in typical Giant fashion, that hope was ripped from the waiting hands of fans as soon as it appeared. The Giants’ 2025 draft class looked excellent early, as the combination of RB Cam Skattebo and QB Jaxson Dart is going to be electric for years to come. Unfortunately, Skattebo suffered a season-ending leg injury and Dart is out indefinitely following a concussion last week. Luckily, #3 overall pickup EDGE Abdul Carter is playing as advertised, and helps support an elite defensive front alongside veterans DT Dexter Lawrence, EDGE Brian Burns, and EDGE Kayvon Thibodeaux. This year is not the Giants’ year, clearly, but there is hope on the horizon for the G-Men.

2025 · Preseason

East #4 · 2024 3-14 · Breakout: QB Jaxson Dart (R)

The G-Men will likely improve on another dismal 2024 campaign under former Coach of the Year Brian Daboll. The Daniel Jones era is officially over in the Big Apple, and Giants fans can finally rejoice as their quarterback purgatory might just be over after drafting Ole Miss QB Jaxson Dart this offseason. Dart SHOULD NOT start at the beginning of this year, and he should instead sit behind veteran journeyman QB Russell Wilson for the first half of the year before getting handed the keys to the castle. The defense, specifically the defensive line, is actually one of the NFL’s best, and should make life hell for Hurts, Daniels, and Prescott for years to come. WR Malik Nabers should be able to build on a stellar rookie season to cement himself as one of the league’s premier pass catchers.

2024 · Midseason

Ranked #25 · 2-7

Another year, another mediocre season for the G-Men when it comes to the Win-Loss column. Put most of the blame for this stat on Head Coach Brian Daboll, as the Giants have lost most of their outings by one score. The investment in quarterback Daniel Jones last offseason has not paid any dividends for the Giants, and Vanilla Vick has once again shown he is just not up to par when it comes to putting his team in position to win. There are bright spots, however. New York's first round pick, LSU receiver Malik Nabers, has shown up as a legitimate WR1 through the first half of the year, and should be a staple in the Giants' offense for the next decade. The Giants defensive line has also been a serious problem for opposing offenses, and their unit currently leads the league in sacks on the back of DPOTY candidate Dexter Lawrence. The pass rushing duo of Lawrence and Brian Burns looks to be among the league's most elite.
