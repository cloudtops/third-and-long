2026 · Preseason

West #4 · 2025 3-14 · Breakout: RB Jeremiyah Love (R)

The Arizona Cardinals are in all-out rebuild mode and should finish towards the bottom of overall league standings in 2026. QB Jacoby Brissett takes the reins as the official starter following a surprisingly efficient 2025 campaign and the departure of franchise stalwart QB Kyler Murray for Minnesota. Third overall pick RB Jeremiyah Love will make an impact early for Arizona, and look for third year WR Marvin Harrison Jr to finally break out now that he has some consistency at the quarterback position. TE Trey McBride is another star piece of this offense, but outside of those names there just isn’t enough for the Cardinals to bank on to really expect any sort of success this year. The defense is plain bad (28th overall last season), and doesn't really have any bright spots outside of veteran S Budda Baker and LB Mack Wilson Jr.

2025 · Midseason

Ranked #23 · 3-6

The Arizona Cardinals seem to have finally moved on from QB Kyler Murray. With the insertion of veteran QB Jacoby Brissett proving to be a better move for the team, and the inability of Murray to really establish any sort of stability in his career outside of a stellar rookie season, it appears a new day is dawning in Phoenix. The team has been a dumpster fire this season, losing multiple winnable games and getting blown out in others. The season is over at the midway point for an Arizona team that some thought may be a dark horse contender, but moving on from Murray is the right move and will allow for Arizona’s front office to develop a different young quarterback for their imminent rebuild.

2025 · Preseason

West #4 · 2024 8-9 · Breakout: WR Marvin Harrison Jr.

Arizona is another perplexing team going into the new season next week. QB Kyler Murray enters his seventh pro season, and the former Heisman winner and #1 overall pick has never returned to the highs of his stellar rookie campaign all those years ago. He is inconsistent, and not a quarterback who can sustain a deep postseason run regardless of the offense around him. This offense is not particularly strong either, outside of WR Marvin Harrison Jr, who should improve on a relatively strong rookie campaign, and TE Trey McBride, who should likely be a Pro Bowler this season. Arizona misses the playoffs again behind another underwhelming campaign from Murray, and a fairly weak defensive unit.

2024 · Midseason

Ranked #21 · 5-4

The Cardinals have probably been the toughest team for me to pin down thus far. One week they will get blown out, and the next week they will look like an offensive juggernaut and have shown the ability to beat good football teams. Quarterback Kyler Murray has quietly been putting together a very solid campaign and should be getting looks for the Pro Bowl if he can continue to play at a high level. Murray is currently 5th in the NFL in QBR and that number has been bolstered this season following the addition of Ohio State WR Marvin Harrison Jr. The question with this team lies in the defense, which has not looked good this year and is among the league's worst statistically. The Cardinals currently hold the lead in the wide open NFC West, but I just do not trust this defense to take this team to playoffs, even if Murray continues to turn heads with his dual-threat abilities.
