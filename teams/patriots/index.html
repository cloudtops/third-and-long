2026 · Preseason

East #2 · 2025 14-3 · Breakout: WR Kyle Williams

After using the genie in the bottle that team owner Robert Kraft found in a Foxborough storage closet back in the year 2000 to wish for multiple Super Bowl trophies and the greatest football player of all time, it seems Kraft has used the genie again to wish for a return to glory. With one of the quickest rebuilds in league history culminating in a Super Bowl appearance last year, New England is one of the most exciting and surprising clubs in football. How long did we have, maybe five years of Patriot irrelevance? QB Drake Maye returns from an MVP-caliber campaign in 2025 and should be much improved behind a better offensive line and the addition of WR AJ Brown. The defense, stalwart in 2025, adds key pieces to the mix in DT Milton Williams and S Kevin Byard III. This year is New England’s opportunity to quell the suspicion that the main reason for their success last season was their schedule.

2025 · Midseason

Ranked #3 · 8-2

The more things change, the more things stay the same. After a half-decade stretch residing in the basement of the AFC, Patriot fans have returned to a land of familiarity at the top of their conference. Strength of scheduling notwithstanding, the Patriots have been soundly defeating the teams in front of them, making up a catalog of wins that includes other contenders in Tampa Bay and Buffalo. HC Mike Vrabel looks like the frontrunner for Coach of the Year, as he has brought the Patriots back to the forefront of the league behind the explosive emergence of second-year signal caller QB Drake Maye. Maye is currently the favorite to win MVP, but the less eye-catching reason for New England’s success is the defense. The Patriots boast the league’s second best scoring defense and have been nothing short of elite against the run.

East #2 · 2024 4-13 · Breakout: QB Drake Maye

The New England Patriots are one of the league’s most revamped teams over the offseason, and look like they’ve made all the right moves in order to put their team in a situation to improve significantly this year. New HC Mike Vrabel will guide this team through one of the league’s easiest schedules to the 7th seed in the AFC Playoffs, as New England becomes the league’s most surprising turnaround in 2025. First round pick OL Will Campbell, as well as rookies RB TreVeyon Henderson and WR Kyle Williams will support QB Drake Maye as he makes a huge leap to stardom in year two.

2024 · Midseason

Ranked #28 · 2-7

Things continue to sputter in Foxborough through the first half of the season. Football fans (especially AFC East fans), can finally breathe a sigh of relief as the team has officially moved on from the Bill Belichick era. He can't hurt you any more, Jets fans. The Patriots looked like an unmitigated disaster until they finally gave the nod to their third overall pick, Duke quarterback Drake Maye. Maye has had a few rookie struggles (as to be expected), but is really coming into his own and looks like he can make all the throws and put his team in position to win. Maye electrified fans with a last second touchdown pass last week against Tennessee, and although the Titans ended up winning in overtime, fans of the Patriots have something to be excited about moving forward. The defense continues to be an issue, which is particularly concerning considering first year Head Coach Jerod Mayo's status as an all-time Patriots linebacking great.
