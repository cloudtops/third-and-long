2026 · Preseason

North #3 · 2025 10-7 · Breakout: WR Germie Bernard (R)

The Mike Tomlin Experience has finally come to a conclusion in the Steel City, after 19 seasons straight finishing above .500. Lack of postseason success led the Steelers front office to pursue someone who they believe can finally get the job done in January, so they hired…wait, Mike McCarthy? Huh. Anyway, McCarthy gets a reunion with his long-lost QB Aaron Rodgers, the ageless wonder who has committed to this year being his farewell tour. He will be supported by WR D.K. Metcalf, RB Jaylen Warren, and TE Pat Freiermuth. Key additions to the offensive squad include last year’s breakout star RB Rico Dowdle and WR Michael Pittman Jr. Pittsburgh looks to rebound defensively from a campaign that saw them ranked towards the basement of the league last season and have added much-needed depth in the box and secondary, headlined by the acquisitions of CB Jalen Ramsey and S Jaquan Brisker.

2025 · Midseason

Ranked #16 · 5-4

Another year in Pittsburgh, and another 9-8 season seems inevitable. QB Aaron Rodgers looked like he had finally found the correct dosage of psychedelics to perform at his 2021 level earlier in the year, but it appears that age has finally caught up to the legendary signal caller. The offense has been decent, but the real problem for Pittsburgh lies on defense. When you’re spending that much money on defense, you need a unit that gets it done, and Pittsburgh’s D has been pretty sub-par through 10 weeks. Factor in the resurgence of the Ravens and a few new faces in the AFC playoff picture, and Pittsburgh is in real danger of missing the playoffs after a hot start to the season.

2025 · Preseason

North #3 · 2024 10-7 · Breakout: RB Kaleb Johnson (R)

The Pittsburgh Steelers will look brand-new on offense for 2025, signing veteran signal caller QB Aaron Rodgers after his tumultuous tenure with the New York Jets. After shipping QB Russell Wilson to the Giants, and QB Justin Fields to the Jets, Rodgers will look to build on the strong finish he had to 2024 in what will likely be his last season in the NFL. WR George Pickens and RB Najee Harris are both gone, and the offense will have to rely on the savvy vet’s experience in order to compete in any capacity in one of football’s toughest divisions. The defense will remain stalwart, but it won’t be enough to squeak into the playoffs for Pittsburgh.

2024 · Midseason

Ranked #9 · 6-2

One of the biggest surprises of the season has been the success of the Pittsburgh Steelers. Mike Tomlin's squad is historic for barely crossing the 8-win threshold, but it seems like this season they are primed for bigger things. Offseason acquisition QB Russell Wilson was an unpopular pickup for the black and gold, but the gamble has paid dividends as Wilson has reverted to his old ways, looking elite through his last two starts. The emergence of third year receiver George Pickens bodes well for Wilson, as Pickens' contested catch ability ranks among the best in the league. Defensive Player of the Year favorite DE TJ Watt continues to move heaven and earth to get to the quarterback, and the reason Pittsburgh has a 6-2 record could be solely attributed to him. The Steelers play in a tough division, but if Tomlin and Wilson can continue to win, they have a good chance at making a run in the playoffs.
