2026 · Preseason

South #3 · 2025 6-11 · Breakout: DE Chase Young

A difficult team to predict for 2026. New Orleans was a bottom-dweller last season, but I see the Saints improving significantly this year as second-year QB Tyler Shough builds on the success he saw after taking over the offense towards the end of 2025. Newly acquired RB Travis Etienne will provide a strong boost in the run game, and WR Chris Olave and rookie WR Jordan Tyson (when he returns from injury) will provide Shough with enough firepower to make a legitimate leap forward. The defense was actually sneaky good towards the tail end of 2025, look for former first round pick DE Chase Young to continue the success he enjoyed at the position this season (finished 2025 with 10 sacks in his last 12 games).

2025 · Midseason

Ranked #30 · 2-8

A tough outlook in the offseason for New Orleans has amounted to a 2-8 mark at the midpoint of the season. QB Spencer Rattler was serviceable through the first nine games, but the lack of winning paved the way for second round pick QB Tyler Shough to take over the starting role. Shough is 1-0 in his career, and led the Saints to victory last week against the Panthers. One feel-good win aside, the Saints are a rebuilding team that will not see success in the next 3 years. The defense has holes, the offense is inexperienced, and the coaching staff has been largely ineffective. Another year at the bottom of the NFC South for the Saints.

2025 · Preseason

South #4 · 2024 3-14 · Breakout: QB Spencer Rattler

Unfortunately for the New Orleans faithful, the Saints are in full rebuild mode following a disastrous 2024 campaign. A quarterback battle will occur throughout 2025, where rookie QB Tyler Shough will be waiting in the wings for starting QB Spencer Rattler to misfire. Rattler actually looked decent through the second half of 2024, and has a lot to build on throughout this upcoming season. The defense is a disaster, and will not be able to get off the field to allow Rattler, RB Alvin Kamara, and WR Chris Olave to make any meaningful contributions to the scoreboard this year. Look for the Saints to compete for the #1 overall pick in April.

2024 · Midseason

Ranked #32 · 2-7

Following a blazing hot 2-0 start, the Saints are coming into Week 10 on the heels of a league-leading 7 game losing streak. This team has been nothing short of a dumpster fire through the first half of the season, coupling an impotent offense with an equally porous defense. Derek Carr has been mediocre when available, and the team was not able to get anything going with their 5th round Oklahoma product Spencer Rattler. One of the lone bright spots in the Big Easy has been emerging superstar receiver Chris Olave, who was recently sidelined with another concussion. Saints faithful are hoping that interim head coach Darren Rizzi can right the ship after Dennis Allen's disastrous tenure, but as things stand at the halfway point, this team will have a top 3 pick in next year's draft.
