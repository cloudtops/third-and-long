2026 · Preseason

West #1 · 2025 12-5 · Breakout: TE Terrance Ferguson

Difficult as it is for me to stomach putting a team that won the Super Bowl at second in their division, HC Sean McVay’s Rams are really the first NFL super team that we have seen constructed in this modern era. One of the best offenses in football last year returns reigning MVP QB Matthew Stafford, OPOTY runner up WR Puka Nacua, WR Davante Adams (14 touchdowns in 2025), and elusive RB Kyren Williams. The offense is elite, but the reason the Rams are so heavily favored to win the Super Bowl in 2026 (5-to-1 odds) are the almost comical additions they’ve made to their already strong defense. DE Myles Garrett, who won DPOTY last year. CBs Jaylen Watson and Trent McDuffie, both former Super Bowl winners from Kansas City. And, of course, the return from retirement of surefire first-ballot Hall of Famer DT Aaron Donald. The Rams are old. I get it. But this is not a team of the future, this is a team of the now, and as far as rosters go, this is the best team in football.

2025 · Midseason

Ranked #2 · 7-2

The other top contender in the NFC West is Sean McVay’s Rams. The Rams are the only other NFL team with no real questions or weaknesses, and they play a true complementary brand of football. The ageless QB Matthew Stafford is quietly having an MVP season, and is leaning on the elite tandem of WRs Puka Nacua and Davante Adams en route to the league-lead for touchdown passes. The offense has been unstoppable lately, and the defense, which looked to be LA’s calling card at the season’s outset, has been as advertised. The opportunistic defense has been making the most of their takeaways, and boasts the league’s fourth-ranked differential in that category. The road to the Super Bowl will go through the NFC West this season, as Seattle and Los Angeles are head and shoulders above the rest of the league at the midpoint.

2025 · Preseason

West #2 · 2024 10-7 · Breakout: DE Braden Fiske

A lot of people are very high on the Rams going into 2025, and their success this year will be contingent on the strength of QB Matthew Stafford’s back. If it holds up throughout the year, Stafford has a very strong offense to pilot, and the addition of WR Davante Adams should lead the Rams to the playoffs. However, Stafford is 37 years old, and back injuries are generally not things that simply vanish. Without Stafford, this offense will struggle. Mightily. Luckily, Los Angeles boasts one of the league’s best defensive fronts, and DE Jared Verse will build on his Defensive Rookie of the Year campaign en route to being in contention for Defensive Player of the Year. Verse is a fitting heir to Aaron Donald’s throne, but don’t sleep on their second year DE on the other side, Braden Fiske.

2024 · Midseason

Ranked #15 · 4-4

After a brutal 1-4 start, the Rams are beginning to look dangerous again. QB Matthew Stafford continues to show why he is one of the league's most underrated signal callers, and with the emergence of veteran receiver DeMarcus Robinson and the return of superstar receiver Cooper Kupp, the Rams look poised for an NFC West crown and an interesting playoff run. The defense looked shaky to start, after losing future Hall of Famer Aaron Donald on the interior, but Los Angeles' ability to draft well at this position over the last two seasons is only making them better. Rookie DE Jared Verse is the early favorite for Defensive Rookie of the Year, and the addition of safety Kam Curl has this defense looking stingy as of late. Head Coach Sean McVay is one of the best in the league for a reason, and the Rams should be in a dogfight with Seattle and San Francisco come December.
