1

Detroit Lions

7-1

Detroit is the NFL's most legitimate juggernaut through the first nine weeks of the season. After a close loss to Tampa Bay in Week 3, it has been all gas no brakes for Detroit and they have blown out pretty much every team they've faced. QB Jared Goff has been the league's most efficient passer and has conducted the offense masterfully, orchestrating blowout after blowout. The loss of DE Aidan Hutchinson hurts for Detroit, as he was on track for one of the greatest seasons for an edge rusher in recent memory. Hutchinson has not played since October 13th and is still top five in the league in QB pressures. The fate of Detroit lies with their Head Coach Dan Campbell, and I believe this year is the year Detroit finally breaks through and wins their first-ever Super Bowl title.

2

Kansas City Chiefs

8-0

It's the same story for Kansas City. Despite facing struggles with injury through the first half of the season, the Chiefs have remained stalwart, and have yet to relinquish a lead this season. With the midseason loss of WR Rashee Rice, the Chiefs receiving corps looked bleak, until the addition of former All Pro receiver DeAndre Hopkins. Through two games with Kansas City, Hopkins has proved crucial for the Chiefs' offense, and Reid's squad looks prime for another deep postseason run. Kansas City is undefeated by way of an elite defense and a ludicrously effective 3rd-down offense, as well as a sneaky punishing rushing offense. QB Patrick Mahomes is looking to become the first quarterback in NFL history to win three straight Super Bowl titles, and with the way the defense is playing, that doesn't seem out of the question.

3

Baltimore Ravens

7-3

The Baltimore Ravens have the NFL's best player, and it hasn't really been close over the last year or so. QB Lamar Jackson, who is coming off of his second career MVP season, has kept the Ravens alive in a season where their defense has been porous, specifically when it comes to opposing quarterbacks throwing the ball. Jackson has been spectacular, and leads the league in the following statistics: QBR, passer rating, touchdown passes, rushing yards (QB). He has thrown 24 touchdown passes to 2 interceptions. Jackson was elite last season, and the offense has only gotten better with the addition of future Hall of Fame running back Derrick Henry. Henry gives the Ravens one of the most dangerous one-two punches in recent memory, and Baltimore is my favorite to win the AFC if they can stay healthy.

4

Buffalo Bills

7-2

Another routine season for Buffalo. The Bills basically had their playoff ticket punched in week 6, and that privilege not only was allowed due to the stink of the rest of their division, but because their QB Josh Allen continues to play at an All-World level. Allen is a dark horse favorite for MVP, and after early season stumbles, the Bills look like their old selves again. The defense has looked pretty mediocre, and that only stands as a testament to how well Allen has been playing through nine weeks. The addition of former Pro Bowler Amari Cooper only helps Buffalo's passing attack following the departures of WR Stefon Diggs and WR Gabe Davis. The only thing that stands in the way of a Super Bowl berth is, wait for it Buffalo fans, Arrowhead Stadium.

5

Philadelphia Eagles

6-2

After a sketchy start, the Eagles look like they have fully returned to their 2022 form. QB Jalen Hurts is playing unbelievably well, and WR AJ Brown's return from injury, as well as the re-emergence of former Heisman Trophy winner DeVonta Smith bodes well for Philadelphia's future to battle for NFC East crown and a first round bye in the playoffs. That being said, the key to Philly's offense is undoubtedly RB Saquon Barkley, as the former division rival has unlocked himself to once again become one of the league's premier backfield threats. Barkley trails only behind Baltimore's Derrick Henry in rushing yards, but leads the league in total yards from scrimmage. Philly's defense ain't no slouch, either. The City of Brotherly Love currently boasts the third best unit in terms of yards per game allowed. If Head Coach Nick Sirianni can establish dominance over the rest of the NFC East, the Eagles should coast to second place in the conference.

6

Washington Commanders

7-2

I will try to keep this unbiased, as a Commanders fan. Without question, the biggest surprise of the 2024 NFL season has been the early success of the Commanders. When new owner Josh Harris and newly appointed GM Adam Peters selected LSU QB Jayden Daniels with the second pick in the draft, they had no idea the efficiency, and stability he would provide to, over the last decade, one of the worst offenses in the league. All Daniels has done through his first nine games is dominate. The Commanders have turned the ball over three times through nine weeks. I'll let that statistic sink in. That's the best mark through nine weeks since 1933, and they're doing it with a rookie quarterback. With the recent addition of Saints All Pro CB Marshon Lattimore, this team is looking to go deep for the first time since 1991, when they went 15-1 and won the Super Bowl. First year Commanders Head Coach Dan Quinn has Washington fans enveloped in an all-encompassing euphoria, and the fast track to Coach of the Year.

7

Minnesota Vikings

6-2

Minnesota started the season out as the league's lone undefeated squad, firing on all cylinders at 5-0 through five weeks. The last three weeks have been tough for Minnesota, after dropping two straight to Detroit and Los Angeles, they rebounded last week, albeit narrowly, against Indianapolis. QB Sam Darnold has been having a career renaissance this season, and that success can likely be chalked up to the presence of WR Justin Jefferson. Once again, we are spouting praise for another LSU receiver, but over the last few seasons, Jefferson has earned every single cent of the contract that the Vikings have given him. He rivals only his former teammate Ja'Marr Chase in explosivity. If the Vikings can continue their offensive production, and Defensive Coordinator Brian Flores' elite game planning, the Vikings can challenge for a top four NFC playoff seed and a deep playoff run.

8

Green Bay Packers

6-3

The Packers gambled in the offseason after giving QB Jordan Love a massive contract extension after a breakout season last year. This season, Love is showing shades of former Packers legend Brett Favre, throwing death-defying touchdown passes balanced out with boneheaded interceptions. Love has the Packers on an inside track to an NFC Wild Card spot, but the real story in Green Bay has been the ability of their defense to generate turnovers. Safety Xavier McKinney leads the NFL in interceptions, and Green Bay looks like a very balanced squad with the ability to go deep into January. The addition of Las Vegas RB Josh Jacobs has been a catalyst for Green Bay's offensive success, as his explosiveness has catapulted the Packers into the top 10.

9

Pittsburgh Steelers

6-2

One of the biggest surprises of the season has been the success of the Pittsburgh Steelers. Mike Tomlin's squad is historic for barely crossing the 8-win threshold, but it seems like this season they are primed for bigger things. Offseason acquisition QB Russell Wilson was an unpopular pickup for the black and gold, but the gamble has paid dividends as Wilson has reverted to his old ways, looking elite through his last two starts. The emergence of third year receiver George Pickens bodes well for Wilson, as Pickens' contested catch ability ranks among the best in the league. Defensive Player of the Year favorite DE TJ Watt continues to move heaven and earth to get to the quarterback, and the reason Pittsburgh has a 6-2 record could be solely attributed to him. The Steelers play in a tough division, but if Tomlin and Wilson can continue to win, they have a good chance at making a run in the playoffs.

10

Houston Texans

6-3

Houston QB CJ Stroud, who had one of the greatest rookie seasons by a quarterback in NFL history, has been experiencing something of a sophomore slump so far in 2024. The losses of WR Nico Collins and WR Stefon Diggs have the Texans in receiver purgatory at the moment, and the buck will fall to second year pass-catcher Tank Dell to carry the boats for Stroud. The addition of Cincinnati RB Joe Mixon has bolstered the Houston rushing attack, and gives them a solid dual-threat attack when moving the football. The reason behind the Texans' 6-3 start, however, has been the defense. Their unit ranks #2 in yards allowed per game, and the pass rush duo of Will Anderson Jr. and Danielle Hunter have been making life difficult for opposing quarterbacks. Former first round pick CB Derek Stingley Jr. is coming on nicely this season as well, and Houston should cruise to an AFC South title.

11

Atlanta Falcons

6-3

Atlanta has had a surprising start to the 2024 season. After signing veteran quarterback Kirk Cousins from Minnesota, the Falcons have finally utilized their recent first round draft picks on offense to turn into one of the league's premier scoring threats. RB Bijan Robinson and TE Kyle Pitts are having breakout seasons, and the addition of Chicago WR Darnell Mooney is allowing Cousins to flourish in the A. Cousins has been balling out, as evidenced by a league-high 509 yard passing performance earlier this year, and his offensive stewardship has Atlanta in the driver's seat in the NFC South. The defense, which has been suspect in past seasons, has been gelling this year behind the solid play of former All Pro cornerback AJ Terrell. Atlanta has been one of the league's best surprises this season.

12

Cincinnati Bengals

4-6

After a typical Cincinnati start, the Bengals look to be figuring things out again. QB Joe Burrow has been playing at a truly elite level, and is probably playing the best football of his career since getting drafted in 2020. The defense, which looked absolutely abysmal at the beginning of the season, has started to gel, at least to the point where Burrow doesn't need to score on every single drive to keep them in the game. WR Ja'Marr Chase is the league's most dangerous deep threat and gives Cincinnati a chance to score on every single offense snap. The loss of RB Joe Mixon has been crucial for the Bengals' rushing attack, but second year RB Chase Brown has been very solid as a replacement and gives them a multifaceted offensive attack. If the Bengals can continue their hot streak as of late, they could make a legitimate push in one of the best divisions in football. A close loss to Baltimore in Week 10 does not sway my opinion on the Bengals being one of the better teams in the league.

13

Tampa Bay Buccaneers

4-5

The story of Tampa Bay this season has been the continued excellence of 6th-year signal caller Baker Mayfield. Mayfield has once again defied the odds and has Tampa Bay's offense firing on all cylinders, even without his two best receivers in future Hall of Famer Mike Evans and veteran Chris Godwin. Mayfield has lit up virtually every defense he has faced up to this point, although he does need to limit his takeaways moving forward. A key facet of the Bucs' success on offense this season has been their surprising ability to run the football. Rookie RB Bucky Irving has been shining so far this season behind Tampa Bay's excellent offensive line, spearheaded by superstar LT Tristan Wirfs. Wirfs has quietly been one of the NFL's best players and is a key reason why Tampa Bay is in the mix for the NFC South title. They may not have a winning record at this point, but their ability to take Kansas City to the wire in primetime last week shows this is a legit squad that could make noise in a stacked NFC.

14

Los Angeles Chargers

5-4

With the hiring of former Michigan Head Coach Jim Harbaugh, this team is finally moving in the right direction. Offseason darling QB Justin Herbert has continued to be a very solid commander of this offense, but he now has a legitimate team around him. The defense has been one of the league's best through nine weeks, holding teams to a league-best 12.6 points/game and just a hair over 300 yards/game, good for eighth in the NFL. DE Khalil Mack is having a renaissance season, and when his counterpart DE Joey Bosa gets back to full strength this defense should look even scarier. Although they may not win their division, I fully expect the Chargers to be in the mix come playoff time and may even play spoiler to their archrivals in Kansas City.

15

Los Angeles Rams

4-4

After a brutal 1-4 start, the Rams are beginning to look dangerous again. QB Matthew Stafford continues to show why he is one of the league's most underrated signal callers, and with the emergence of veteran receiver DeMarcus Robinson and the return of superstar receiver Cooper Kupp, the Rams look poised for an NFC West crown and an interesting playoff run. The defense looked shaky to start, after losing future Hall of Famer Aaron Donald on the interior, but Los Angeles' ability to draft well at this position over the last two seasons is only making them better. Rookie DE Jared Verse is the early favorite for Defensive Rookie of the Year, and the addition of safety Kam Curl has this defense looking stingy as of late. Head Coach Sean McVay is one of the best in the league for a reason, and the Rams should be in a dogfight with Seattle and San Francisco come December.

16

Denver Broncos

5-4

The Broncos are another club that bet on a young quarterback early in the draft, and this gamble has appeared to be paying off for them thus far. Oregon QB Bo Nix has had his up and downs through nine weeks, but has recently been showing why Denver took the chance on him by limiting turnovers and showing legitimate toughness running the football. Veteran WR Courtland Sutton is developing a strong chemistry with Nix, and they have recently been doing a much better job of running the football. The real story in Denver lies on the other side of the ball, however. The Broncos' defense has been elite so far this year, and CB Patrick Surtain II is a big reason why this team is off to a surprising start, and has a chance for a berth in the AFC playoffs. Denver's defense is currently holding teams to 17.9 points/game, good for third in the league, and this is the main reason they find themselves in the top half of the rankings.

17

Seattle Seahawks

4-5

Seattle has been a bit of an enigma this season. Following a 3-0 start, the Seahawks have regressed on both sides of the ball. Veteran journeyman QB Geno Smith is one of the league's true gunslingers, and his boom-or-bust play style has both won and lost games for Seattle this season. Superstar WR DK Metcalf's absence has also been a significant blow to the offense, as a knee sprain has limited their offense's ability to crack big plays, as Metcalf was among the league's premier deep threats before his injury. After a brilliant start from the defensive unit, injuries have severely impacted their ability to make stops, although rookie DT Byron Murphy Jr. has shown up for Seattle as one of the league's premier young interior defensive linemen. Second year corner Devon Witherspoon continues to be one of the most exciting defensive players to watch in the NFC, and expect one of the league's youngest teams to evolve into a legitimate threat in the next few seasons.

18

San Francisco 49ers

4-4

A team that should climb on this list as the season continues, the 49ers have gone from a top three team in the league last season to an average- to- mediocre squad. An injury-riddled roster and regression from their young quarterback Brock Purdy are mostly to blame for their sluggish start after a loss in the Super Bowl last season. Reigning Offensive Player of the Year Christian McCaffrey has still yet to take a snap in San Francisco's backfield, and although RB Jordan Mason has been carrying the torch well in CMC's absence, the team seems to be missing the spark that has propelled them deep into the postseason recently. The defense remains a top 10 unit, and the 49ers should continue to move up this list if Shanahan can figure things out offensively.

19

Indianapolis Colts

4-5

Arguably ranked too high in this list, Indianapolis has been another very strange team this season. One week they look solid, and the next week they look hapless. Most of the blame for their average start this year can be placed on the shoulders of second year quarterback Anthony Richardson. Richardson can dazzle at times, but is statistically one of the worst quarterbacks of the last 30 years in terms of quarterback rating and completion percentage in NFL history. His raw athleticism and bruising running style strikes fear in opposing defenses but also has led to numerous injuries over his career and this season is no different. Following his benching, the Colts have inserted veteran QB Joe Flacco, and the offense looks like more of the same following the switch. The Colts looks like no threat in the AFC, but could vie for a wild card berth if things fall their way.

20

Chicago Bears

4-4

There are few fates in life I can imagine that are as cruel as being a Bears fan. After drafting "generational" USC QB Caleb Williams with the first overall pick of the draft, and adding WR Keenan Allen, the Bears had the splashiest offseason of any team and looked to be in a position to make a playoff run. All of this depended on the speed of Williams' acclimatization to the pros, and the results have been, well, mixed. After a 4-2 start where Williams had started to look elite, Chicago has crashed back down to earth, following a gut-wrenching loss on a hail mary and a blowout in back to back weeks. The team appears to have quit on Head Coach Matt Eberflus, and the calls for his firing from the Chicago faithful are deafening. The bright spot so far has been the defense, which looked like the NFL's most stalwart unit through the first seven weeks of the season but has regressed recently. Expect the Bears to finish last in the league's best division.

21

Arizona Cardinals

5-4

The Cardinals have probably been the toughest team for me to pin down thus far. One week they will get blown out, and the next week they will look like an offensive juggernaut and have shown the ability to beat good football teams. Quarterback Kyler Murray has quietly been putting together a very solid campaign and should be getting looks for the Pro Bowl if he can continue to play at a high level. Murray is currently 5th in the NFL in QBR and that number has been bolstered this season following the addition of Ohio State WR Marvin Harrison Jr. The question with this team lies in the defense, which has not looked good this year and is among the league's worst statistically. The Cardinals currently hold the lead in the wide open NFC West, but I just do not trust this defense to take this team to playoffs, even if Murray continues to turn heads with his dual-threat abilities.

22

Dallas Cowboys

3-5

I'd like to state my bias early in this one… nothing has made me happier this season than the implosion of the Dallas Cowboys. The departure of running back Tony Pollard has crippled the offense, as the Cowboys' offense has been the definition of a one-trick pony, asking All Pro receiver CeeDee Lamb to carry the team on his back. Lamb has again been elite this year, but QB Dak Prescott has been seeing ghosts this year and continues to make head-scratching play after head-scratching play as the offense continues to sputter. The defense has also been alarmingly mediocre this season, as the absence of superstar linebacker Micah Parsons has severely crippled this unit and the Cowboys look to be in freefall over the last few weeks, losing their last three contests. Expect Head Coach Mike McCarthy to get the axe if things continue to go the way they've been going in JerryWorld.

23

New York Jets

3-6

The Jets have been, in my opinion, the league's most disappointing team so far this season. The return of four time MVP Aaron Rodgers following an achilles tear on the fourth play of the season last year, coupled with an elite defense had the Jets faithful hopeful for a deep playoff run. Things have gone the opposite way so far this year. Losses have just kept piling up for New York following a 2-1 start, and Rodgers appears to be showing his age as the league's oldest player. A marquee win against Houston in primetime last week has some folks believing in the Jets again, but if the offense continues to look as inept as it has so far this year, the Jets will have a top 10 pick in the draft come April. It's not all bad in the Big Apple, though, as second year receiver Garrett Wilson is emerging into a legitimate superstar and Rodgers' favorite target, which is a testament to his skill as Rodgers' former teammates Allen Lazard and Davante Adams are catching passes for New York as well. The Jets could make things interesting down the stretch if the defense continues to be elite and Rodgers can continue to gel with his guys.

24

Miami Dolphins

2-7

A very disappointing start to the season for Mike McDaniel's ball club. Following yet another scary concussion to QB Tua Tagovailoa, the Dolphins were essentially dead in the water and looked to be one of the league's worst offenses, with the stable of backup QBs Snoop Huntley, Skylar Thompson, and Tim Boyle all failing to effectively utilize all-world receiver Tyreek Hill. Significant injuries to the defense have really kneecapped them as well, and Miami looked like they were essentially finished for the year. Enter Tua. After losing a close game to Arizona in his return, the Dolphins went toe to toe with the Bills last week and only lost on a 61-yard field goal from Buffalo kicker Tyler Bass. Miami might not be in a position to make a playoff push, but with a wide open wild card picture in the AFC, anything is possible. After all, this is the same team that scored 70 points in a single game last season.

25

New York Giants

2-7

Another year, another mediocre season for the G-Men when it comes to the Win-Loss column. Put most of the blame for this stat on Head Coach Brian Daboll, as the Giants have lost most of their outings by one score. The investment in quarterback Daniel Jones last offseason has not paid any dividends for the Giants, and Vanilla Vick has once again shown he is just not up to par when it comes to putting his team in position to win. There are bright spots, however. New York's first round pick, LSU receiver Malik Nabers, has shown up as a legitimate WR1 through the first half of the year, and should be a staple in the Giants' offense for the next decade. The Giants defensive line has also been a serious problem for opposing offenses, and their unit currently leads the league in sacks on the back of DPOTY candidate Dexter Lawrence. The pass rushing duo of Lawrence and Brian Burns looks to be among the league's most elite.

26

Cleveland Browns

2-7

Jameis Winston fans rejoice, for our glorious king has returned to us again. Browns quarterback Deshaun Watson has officially been ruled out for the season following an achilles tear, and this is the lone reason the Browns are among my highest rated 2-7 teams. Watson has been the NFL's worst quarterback this season not named Anthony Richardson, and it hasn't been particularly close. The team appeared to have quit on him, as the Browns continued to get blown out week in and week out before his injury. Following backup QB Jameis Winston's insertion into the lineup, the Browns beat the Ravens. This team still has fight, and their once-vaunted defense looks to have been energized with the offense's emergence. Once former All-Pro running back Nick Chubb gets rolling again, this team is looking to play spoiler for some of their friends in the AFC North.

27

Jacksonville Jaguars

2-7

The Jacksonville Jaguars have been one of the league's most disappointing clubs this season. Hopes were high to start things off this year, and the team believed this was the year former first overall pick Trevor Lawrence made the jump from average to elite. That has not been the case. Lawrence has not been anything special this season, and although he has been consistently let down by his receiving corps (the Jags lead the league in dropped passes through the last three seasons), their quarterback has not been good enough to lead this team to the playoffs. This is even considering the emergence of rookie LSU receiver Brian Thomas Jr., who leads all rookies in receiving yards and continues the trend of LSU receivers just being better than everyone else on the field. The end of the Doug Pederson era seems imminent in Duval, and although the team has looked scrappier over the last few weeks since their 0-5 start, the Jags retain their place among the NFL's bottom feeders.

28

New England Patriots

2-7

Things continue to sputter in Foxborough through the first half of the season. Football fans (especially AFC East fans), can finally breathe a sigh of relief as the team has officially moved on from the Bill Belichick era. He can't hurt you any more, Jets fans. The Patriots looked like an unmitigated disaster until they finally gave the nod to their third overall pick, Duke quarterback Drake Maye. Maye has had a few rookie struggles (as to be expected), but is really coming into his own and looks like he can make all the throws and put his team in position to win. Maye electrified fans with a last second touchdown pass last week against Tennessee, and although the Titans ended up winning in overtime, fans of the Patriots have something to be excited about moving forward. The defense continues to be an issue, which is particularly concerning considering first year Head Coach Jerod Mayo's status as an all-time Patriots linebacking great.

29

Tennessee Titans

2-6

We now move in to the tier of teams where there are some legitimate positives to speak about! Very exciting. The Titans are currently the owners of the NFL's best defense in total yards against. This unit has quietly excelled this season, and like Las Vegas before them, any sort of offensive production would undoubtedly put this squad towards the middle of the pack. Things have been disastrous offensively, however. Second year Kentucky product Will Levis has been atrocious this season, and Mason Rudolph hasn't been anything special in relief of Levis' recent shoulder sprain. The Titans are another team that is severely lacking in the playmaker department. Tony Pollard was shipped to Tennessee from Dallas in the offseason and has been underwhelming in his role as the Titans' leading back. Expect the Titans to grind out a few more wins this season in a poor division on the back of their surprisingly stingy defense.

30

Las Vegas Raiders

2-7

Not many positives to speak on so far through 9 weeks of Raider football. Their league-average defense has been the team's calling card this season, but the blame for their 2-7 record ultimately falls to poor coaching and execution on the offensive side of the ball. Washington State legend Gardner Minshew has been inconsistent, and the Raiders are desperate for a playmaker for him to get the ball to. The losses of Josh Jacobs and Davante Adams have crippled this offense, although rookie tight end Brock Bowers currently leads all tight ends in receiving yards and looks like a future perennial Pro Bowler. Defensive Player of the Year candidate Maxx Crosby is essentially carrying this team on his back right now, but it has not been enough. Another lost season for the hapless Raiders.

31

Carolina Panthers

2-7

In a (relatively) shocking turn of events, the Carolina Panthers do not take the honor of the league's worst team. Following a close win over the Saints last week, last year's first overall pick Bryce Young has his first ever NFL win in the books. The Andy Dalton experiment was an explosive failure, and as it stands the Panthers have currently been outscored by a league-worst -146 point differential through 9 weeks. Owner David Tepper has refused to add any legitimate veteran presences to his team over the last year and it is showing on the field, where the Panthers only miss out on the last spot in these rankings after a close win last week.

32

New Orleans Saints

2-7

Following a blazing hot 2-0 start, the Saints are coming into Week 10 on the heels of a league-leading 7 game losing streak. This team has been nothing short of a dumpster fire through the first half of the season, coupling an impotent offense with an equally porous defense. Derek Carr has been mediocre when available, and the team was not able to get anything going with their 5th round Oklahoma product Spencer Rattler. One of the lone bright spots in the Big Easy has been emerging superstar receiver Chris Olave, who was recently sidelined with another concussion. Saints faithful are hoping that interim head coach Darren Rizzi can right the ship after Dennis Allen's disastrous tenure, but as things stand at the halfway point, this team will have a top 3 pick in next year's draft.
