The Nirv Column

By Noah Irving/2026 Week 2

Come one, come all! And welcome back to another installment of the Nirv column.

Odds are you’re here again due to our riveting breakdown of mascots last week…

Unless you’re a new visitor. In that case, welcome!

Guys and gals, it is yet again an honor to be sharing this space with you to get my ideas out.

Something tells me this column will be a bit different than last week’s…

More football focused, football heavy, and football directed.

Let’s jump into it.

See what I did there? Don’t be a coward taking the points, take ’em straight up.

Now

Week 2 proved to be pretty devastating for most. And I’m not just talking Vandy x NC State….prayers out to the Wolfpack. Ouch. I haven’t been on the wrong side of a late finish like that since [REDACTED].

I’m talking the insane amount of injuries around the league. Caleb Williams, JD5, Jaxson Dart, the list goes on and on. And I get it, plenty of jokes to be made regarding the optics of the Caleb injury. But I’ve never been one to root or joke about injuries, so it’s all the same song to me. Injuries suck, derail the season, and a lot of the time have some pretty meaningful impact on the player moving forward. They just suuuuuuck.

So today for you all, I’d like to keep things lighthearted. You really thought I’d be talking More football focused, football heavy, football directed? On the contrary, friends. Today we’re gonna be talking about something to get our minds off a dislocated elbow, a blown out knee, or a booboo on Soldier Field.

We’re talkin’ sports nicknames.

I get it, I get it- we play for the name on the front of the jersey, not on the back. Who cares about a name, let alone a nickname? I implore you to keep an open mind on this journey with me, because we’ve seen some absolute haymakers when it comes to giving our guys a catchy name. And hell no, I’m not just talkin’ football.

Let’s start with an easy one for the people: Charles Barkley. Goooooood ol’ Chuck. Menace on the court and just a liability on the mic with the NBA TNT crew (shoutout Ernie and those beaches in Galveston, TX). But we aren’t talkin’ Charles or Chuck here, folks. We’re talking the ROUND MOUND OF REBOUND.

Not only does this get points for rhyming….but there are 22 other players in the NBA that averaged more rebounds per game than him. Kinda funny to include rebounding in his nickname…..but that’s only half of it.

Chuck was a big boy. No secret there. Hence the ‘Round Mound’ portion of the name. According to our stats department, Chuck played anywhere between 250 – 285 lbs during his NBA career. I know what you’re thinking, that makes Zion look like Paris Hilton. But Chuck famously GAINED weight, about 50lbs, before the ’84 Draft to avoid being taken by the 76ers. They didn’t care, more of him to love type situation and took him anyway. Got all big and fat for no reason; relatable Mr. Round Mound. And just one more shoutout to those women in San Antonio.

We’ll switch it up a bit and go with a football one: Chad Johnson. Ocho-Cinco. 85.

Listen, I’m biased but I am a massive fan of Ocho-Cinco. The player, the person, his love for Mickey D’s, he’s got it all. And the fact that this ‘nickname’ was technically (legally) his real name for a bit there? Legendary stuff.

A bit of background; in 2008 while he was on the Bengals, Chad (feels weird just saying his first name) tried to throw a temporary nameplate on the back of his jersey that said ‘Ocho-Cinco’ in honor of Hispanic Heritage Month. Velcro and all. The No Fun League didn’t like the gesture and fined Mr. Johnson / Cinco for the little stunt. And that is exactly where they lost the plot. Now I’m not one to judge, but by all accounts, Chad was a….petty person. So naturally, in response to the fine, he decided to legally change his last name to Ocho-Cinco. For those still wondering, he wore 85, Ocho-Cinco….you get it.

But yeah, legit legally changed his name and all. It kinda bummed me out to learn he changed it back to Johnson in 2012 when he was about to get married….but don’t you worry, he kept Ocho-Cinco as his middle name. And kinda like Kobe, he had a hell of a career both BNC (before name change) and ANC (after name change). Names may change but talent doesn’t, and for that we salute you 85.

Speaking of Kobe….

And now moving on to yet another beautiful sport, hockey. Before I get into this one, quick honorable mention for our guy ‘WiFi’, Arber Xhekaj. Last name looks like a default router password, gets me every time.

But we’re here to talk about The Great One, the honorable Wayne Gretzky. Now I feel like this one doesn’t require too much ’splainin, so I’m just gonna give you some of the craziest Wayno stats I’ve come across:

  • If Gretzky never scored a goal, he still would’ve recorded 11 straight 100-point seasons and won four scoring titles
  • If you chopped Gretzky’s entire career numbers in half, he’d still be 17th all-time in points
  • Gretzky is the only player to record a 200-point season, and he did it four times
  • Gretzky could’ve ended his career with a 1,370-game pointless streak and still would’ve been a point-per-game player
  • If you only counted Gretzky’s seasons after turning 30, he’d still rank fourth all-time in points per game

I could go on for hours about what this man accomplished on the ice. To be fair, I feel like The Great One is even underselling him a bit as a nickname.

Now I know what you’re thinking- Nirv, this is a FOOTBALL platform. Talk about FOOTBALL.
