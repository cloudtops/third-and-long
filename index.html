Three Dynasty Prospects to Watch for in 2027

By Daniel Brents

Power Rankings

After Week 5

Please, no one make fun of me. I’ve been through enough with my dogshit team this week. I’m not going to say anything about the Commanders-Giants game in this recap, nobody needs to relive that. If you really care that much I wrote a whole article about it (read it here, you masochists).

Anyway, a whole lot of moving and shaking going on in the big leagues this weekend. Upsets, blowouts, a few really exciting close games, and the most ridiculously idiotic finish to a football game you’ll see this decade. Wait, no, I said I’m not talking about that. Let’s talk about all the teams that actually know how to play the sport of football!

We start off with the funniest thing I can imagine, which was the Cowboys getting completely overwhelmed by the formerly 0-4 Buccaneers and their UDFA rookie QB Jalon Daniels in JerryWorld. This Cowboys team will not win a game unless their offense scores 30+, their defense is that awful. Christian Parker, your days are numbered in Dallas. Anyway, you can read more about Jalon (Yes, Jalon. Not Jayden. Shut up about it already!!!!) Daniels and his performance in this week’s Rookie Radar. On an equally hilarious note, the Eagles crossed the pond and blew a lead with 0:06 remaining, falling to the London Jaguars and continuing their slide into mediocrity. The Post-Sirianni Era is rapidly approaching. On to the NFC North, where the Packers beat up on the rival Bears to the tune of 31-7. Jordan Love was the best player in football this weekend, and looked like the player we saw in 2025, not whoever the hell he is so far this season. Tyson Bagent was atrocious, and this was probably the most unfortunate Sunday of Ben Johnson’s tenure in the Windy City to this point. Texans-Titans was a snoozefest, but CJ Stroud and the Texans got their first win of the season against what just might be the worst team to play football this decade. Cam Ward, I am your strongest soldier, and just wrote an entire article LAST WEEK about why people shouldn’t give up on you, but this is getting to the point where I cannot possibly keep defending the product on the field. I looked at the schedule and I honestly think it’s more likely than not that Tennessee finishes 0-17. My sincerest condolences, Jeremiah Smith.

Cincy vs. Miami is next, a game that actually was closer than initially expected. I’m not going to blow smoke up your ass here, I didn’t watch this game. Can you blame me? Cincinnati gets a close W to improve to 3-2, along with every other team in the AFC North, and also every other team in the NFC North! Fun stat. We’re Fun Stats guys here at 3rd & Long. I was disappointed to see the Raiders get boat raced by the Patriots, it appears the Silver & Black’s 3-0 start was simply a Vegas mirage. Drake Maye has now officially retired from Jersey Mike’s and is back to playing MVP-level football. That was really weird there for a little bit. Down in the Big Easy the Saints dropped their third straight home game to the Minnesota Vikings, who improved to 5-0 and actually got solid offensive production from their offense, not their kicker Will Reichard. Defensive coordinator Brian Flores blitzed Tyler Shough and the Saints at a 85.2% rate. I don’t even need to say anything else. Read that again.

The New York Jets defeated the Cleveland Browns, who remembered that they are the Cleveland Browns and as such, suck at playing football. WR Denzel Boston looks great, so you have that going for you. Which is nice. Anyway, the main story here is that Jets rookie LB David Bailey, the number two overall pick in the draft, is solidly NOT a bust after registering 3.0 separate quarterback takedowns against future Walter Payton Man of the Year Award-winner QB Deshaun Watson. Indy and Pittsburgh played in Western PA, where I was surprised and delighted to learn that the Colts’ record when traveling to the Steel City was 1-17 since the AFL-NFL merger in 1970. It is now 1-18, as QB Aaron Rodgers and RB Jaylen Warren actually have this offense rolling for multiple weeks in a row. Looking at the schedule, 7-2 for Pittsburgh does not seem out of the realm of possibility. In the nation’s capital, the Commanders battled the Giants, and xxxx xxxxxxxxx xxxx xxxxxx xxxxxxx xxxx xxxxxxxxxx xxxx xxxxxxx xxxxx xxxxxxxx xxx xxxxxxxxx

On to the late window! Denver improved to 3-2 against the hapless Chargers, who fell to 0-5 on the year. Not all sunshine and daisies in the Rockies, though, as Denver needs to start learning how to score points in the first half or else they are going to miss the playoffs. QB Bo Nix is missing wide open receivers downfield in order to throw checkdowns to running backs (RB RJ Harvey leads the Broncos in receptions through five weeks), and needs to figure it out quickly. Detroit beat the piss out of the Cardinals, and QB Jared Goff quietly continued an under-the-radar MVP campaign by throwing another three touchdown passes with zero interceptions (He has not thrown a pick this season). RB Jahmyr Gibbs went for 200+ from scrimmage, and WR Jameson Williams scored twice. Ben Johnson who? This offense can still roll! In what was my game of the week until Monday night, the 49ers came to town and got gifted a division win over the archrival Seahawks. #1 vs. #3 in my power rankings, and this game lived up to all of the hype. Seattle honestly played much better than San Francisco overall, but four (!!) costly turnovers cost the Hawks a chance to spoil the Niners’ undefeated record. WR Jaxon Smith-Njigba is the best player in the NFL by such a hilariously wide margin that he’s still the best player in the NFL even when he only has one leg. I expect another great game between these teams when they meet again later on this season, and hopefully a playoff classic in January. Sunday night saw the Falcons fall to the Ravens in a great Bird Bowl that went to overtime, where QB Snoop Huntley played the hero for Baltimore and QB Tua Turndaballova played the fool for Atlanta. I imagine that if QB Michael Penix Jr. wouldn’t have hurt his rib that Atlanta would’ve got the win, but you know, horseshoes and hand grenades or whatever.

You can read all about Monday night’s Game of the Week here. Thanks for reading and following along with us for another week of football. We are starting to gain some real traction here at 3rd & Long, and we greatly appreciate all of your support. We know you have a million places to get your NFL takes, and we thank you very much for choosing to follow along with us! Eat a dick, Portnoy!
