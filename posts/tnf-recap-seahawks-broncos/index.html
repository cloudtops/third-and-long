Thursday Night Recap

Death, Taxes, Thursday Night Football

By Adam Long/Week 6

In football, as in life, we can be sure of but a few things. Death, taxes. Thursday night football, terrible offensive outputs. Another absolute TNF stinker brought to you courtesy of our overlords at Amazon Prime. I hate to say it, as the man is truly a legend, but it might be time for Al Michaels to hang up the headset. The voice is iconic, but unfortunately as Al confronts the twilight of what is truly a Hall of Fame career, things have been shaky in the booth to say the least. But this is a football website, and you came here to hear about football! Not octogenarians. If you wanted that, you’d watch C-SPAN! Ba-doom-tss. Anyone? No? Alright, I’ll see myself out.

I digress; on to the ball. We talk BALL here at 3rd & Long. The reigning, defending Super Bowl champion Seattle Seahawks journeyed to the Mile High City to take on the Denver Broncos to kick off Week 6. The product on the field was an utter snoozefest for the vast majority of the contest, unless you’re one of those sick bastards that loves defense. I bet you root for ties, too. You disgust me. Anyway, both of these quarterbacks are not very good right now. I don’t think anyone would argue with me on that. Denver QB Bo Nix genuinely looks like a high school player, and Seattle QB Sam Darnold has once again reverted to full Pumpkin Mode. Way to get in the Halloween spirit, Sammy! Both offenses experienced acute anemia for essentially this entire game, save for two first half touchdown runs by Bronco stallions RB RJ Harvey and RB JK Dobbins. If there’s anything to know about the Denver running back room, it’s that these guys hate first names. The Broncos were actually able to get players into the end zone in the first half (they entered Thursday’s contest with 23 total first half points this SEASON), and went into the locker room ahead 14-3. The defense was absolutely rocking, led by edge rusher Nik Bonitto, who is still largely underrated and is solidly in that highest, elite tier of sack artists in our league. Even without their secondary’s two best players, CB Patrick Surtain II and S Talanoa Hufanga, Denver’s defense looked more similar to their 2025 unit, which was one of the stoutest in the NFL.

Seattle’s offense wasn’t able to get in a rhythm until the fourth quarter, when Seahawks offensive coordinator Brian Fleury finally remembered that they have the best player in the entire league wearing #11 for their offense. In a down game for the Emerald City Route Artist, WR Jaxon Smith-Njigba still had 12 receptions for 134 yards. Brian. Just force the ball to him every single play. He is uncoverable. Don’t run the ball. Don’t trust Darnold to make a read. Just throw it to JSN every single time, and I promise you that these offensive woes will abate. I know that sounds facetious, but I’m being completely serious. RB George Holani exorcised his Week 5 demons by not fumbling, and also scoring a clutch touchdown in the fourth quarter. WR Rashid Shaheed made a dazzling, highlight-reel 2-Point Conversion catch in the back of the end zone to cut the Bronco lead to three. Shades of Omar Cooper Jr. vs. Penn State last year.

Seattle bounced back again from a late would-be game-sealing interception by the Denver defense and kicked a field goal to send the game to extra innings. Take notes Dan Quinn! After receiving (and being penalized on) the kickoff, Seattle had a solid drive going until Darnold promptly threw another boneheaded interception. Nix and the Broncos ran a conservative three plays, and then K Will Lutz split the uprights for the game winner to seal a 17-14 Denver victory. Shoutout to our own Daniel Brents, the only dissenting opinion in our pregame predictions, who (almost) hit the nail on the head with his score prediction.

So where do both teams go from here? Denver is sitting at 4-2, second in their division, and are allowed to be thrilled with a tight victory over the defending champs. But Broncos fans, are you really convinced this is a team that can go deep into January? I get it, defense wins championships. And this is absolutely a defense that can win you a championship. But man, Nix is seeing ghosts out there. He’s averaging less air yards per attempt than any other quarterback in the league. I’m going to need to see a real explosion from Nix (pause) before I really start drinking that Denver Kool-Aid. Seattle falls to 3-3, and it’s a similar story for them. Their defense is outstanding, and is also a unit that can bring a second Lombardi in as many years to the Pacific Northwest. But Seahawks fans, do you stay the course with Darnold? The man brought you to the top of the mountain last year, but I don’t know a single fan that’s not infuriated with his play this season. Seven interceptions in 4.1 games in 2026 is not a stat that inspires confidence. At least Drew Lock was out there slinging it.

Editor’s note: Holy shit, how did I not talk about the most important part of this whole game. The uniforms!!! These are probably the two best throwback duds in the game. NFL, I hereby petition you to make every team wear throwback jerseys every single week. Unless you’re the Steelers. Don’t think we forgot about the bumblebees. Yuck.
