The Complete Guide to Super Mario World (SNES)

By Noah Irving/Week 1

Alright you cool cats and kittens, welcome to your all-in-one Super Mario World walkthrough. Not to milk my own cow here, but I’ve put in ungodly amounts of time into this game, so I’d like to think I have some knowledge that I can share with the masses.

TO START: tackle Yoshi’s Island 1, and make your way up to the Yellow Switch Palace. This will just fill in some gaps of missing blocks you’ll inevitably encounter on your journey to taking down Bowser.

After that, continue through the game path as normal, and take down that first castle (shoutout Iggy). Keep it pushin until you get to Donut Plains 2. You’ll see in the Donut Plains area, a lot of the levels are a red dot instead of yellow. This means there are actually 2 ways to beat the level; one that will progress you as normal, and another that will lead you to some sort of secret area. Donut Plains 2 will be the first secret area you really want to unlock; the Green Switch Palace. Same as the yellow, but if you encounter one in your journey, it’ll give you a feather. Woo!

IMMEDIATELY AFTER DONUT PLAINS 2, the Donut Ghost House. Arguably the most important level in the entire game. This one also has a secret way to beat the level. You’ll need a cape; just fly up and to the left when you first start the level. Walk along the platform at the top of the screen, go through the ticker tape….and you’ve just unlocked the Top Secret Area. Free mushies, fire flowers, feathers, and Yoshi’s. As much. As. You. Want.

Alright….now that that’s done with, keep moving forward through the game. You’ll eventually get inside the cave to Vanilla Dome 1. Beat it normal, then on Vanilla Dome 2, you’ll want the secret beat to unlock the Red Switch Palace. Keep going through (the normal beat) of Vanilla Dome 2, all the way through castle #3. Go through Cheese Bridge and Cookie Mountain, slay the beast that is Ludwig in castle #4, and make your way to…..the Forest of Illusion….

Not gonna lie, y’all are on your own in the forest. Just like how I had to suffer my way through it, you get to too. Just don’t forget to grab the Blue Switch Palace ;)

Now that you’ve made it through, your reward is Chocolate Island. One of the better islands, especially considering the geopolitical climate…but it is not without its checkered past. Make your way through and conquer Wendy, and now the fun begins. The Valley of Bowser…

We’ll leave it at that for this week. Maybe next time I’ll give you the rest of the walkthrough, maybe it’ll be a different game. Maybe I won’t be invited back as a guest writer. Life is a beautiful thing….
