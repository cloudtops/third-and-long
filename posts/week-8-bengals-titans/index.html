Game of the Week

Cincinnati 43, Tennessee 36

By Adam Long/Week 8

Tennessee Titans1-7
vs
Cincinnati Bengals5-2
Paycor Stadium, Cincinnati, Ohio

A Big XII football game broke out in the NFL this weekend in Cincinnati. Instead of doing my usual play by play recap of everything that went down in this instant classic, I might spend a little time talking about both teams as a whole. How this game in particular went for them, where they’re at, and their outlook for the future. Let’s dive in and start with the team that won this game, the Cincinnati Bengals.

OK, Who Dey nation. You got the win. Nice work! But do you have that old, familiar feeling? The same one that’s crept on you the last half-decade? That you need to rely on your offense to score 40+ to have any chance to win with this defense? Alright alright, I know things are better in Cincy on the defensive side of the ball, especially with the addition of DT Dexter Lawrence II. But yeesh, giving up 36 to the Titans in a shootout and making Cam Ward look like prime Mahomes? Not great. Hopefully just a blip on this unit’s radar, as they have been overachieving compared to expectations pretty considerably this season. The pass rush and run stopping have been elite, so I do believe that this performance was simply an outlier in their overall defensive output thus far. DE Boye Mafe has been spectacular and is among the best edge rushers in football through eight weeks.

So now that the boring part is out of the way (Sorry, Sexy Dexy. I still love ya.), let’s talk about the fun part! This Bengals offense is B-A-C-K. Yeah, Green Bay, Ja’Marr and Tee heard you talking. “We have the best receiver duo in the league! We’re going to win it all this year!” Well, no, you don’t, and no, you aren’t. The NFL is simply more fun when #1 and #5 for Cincy are going bar for bar and both putting up 100+ yards per game. How about we add in a sprinkle of TE Mike Gesicki getting busy in the end zone so far this year? He is currently tied with Mr. Chase et al. at second in the league in touchdown receptions this season. This air-raid passing attack is no surprise, of course, when you look at the man driving the Bengal bus under center. Cincinnati QB Joe Burrow is my MVP pick halfway through the year, and he was also my MVP pick in the preseason. Coincidence? I think not. Burrow is all the way back, and leads the league in touchdown passes to this point in the year. He is on pace to sniff 50 (!) touchdown passes, a feat which has not been accomplished since Mahomes in 2018, and only two other times in league history. He is absolutely lighting it up this season. Burrow threw for 450+ yards in this one to go along with six total touchdowns, and shook off three pretty silly interceptions to a ball-hawking Titans defense. Actually, not the whole defense, just safety Amani Hooker, who had all three picks for Tennessee’s defense.

That’s really the only bright spot for Tennessee defensively. The loss of All-Pro DT Jeffery Simmons really hurts this team. The Titans defense allowed over 500 yards to this Bengals offense, but the only reason this game was even close was due to the offensive explosion from much-maligned QB Cam Ward. I am a staunch Cam Ward defender, and Sunday was the first time I have been vindicated in that regard. Ward played the best game of his career, throwing for 300+ yards along with two scores and piloting the Titan offense to 440 yards of total offense. Ward looked competent under center, and finally was able to capitalize on the natural ability he has to extend off-script plays. Tennessee RB Tony Pollard has been a nightmare for opposing defenses as well recently, scoring five touchdowns and rushing for nearly 250 yards in his last two games.

So, what’s the outlook? I firmly believe Cincinnati is a Super Bowl contender in a relatively weak AFC. If this offense can look half this good week in and week out, Burrow has a chance to make good on his promise to finally bring a Lombardi to Cincinnati. For Tennessee, things are definitely looking better. Ward has looked less lost as of late, and the offense is starting to gel. Lots of draft capital for Titans fans to look forward to in the coming years, so the outlook is pretty far from bleak. Just keep holding the line and improving on what you are doing well, Robert Saleh. There are brighter days ahead.
