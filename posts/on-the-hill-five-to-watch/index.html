On the Hill

Five to Watch

By Henry Houghton/Week 6

About halfway through the late-slate games on Sunday, Scott Hanson confirmed we are already 25% of the way through the 2026-27 NFL season. Crazy right? With the season flying by and your fantasy team already 0-4, it’s probably a smart idea to start looking at some 2027 mock drafts and see how some of our top prospects are shaping up this college season. Here are 5 guys you should keep on your radar over the next few weeks:

Arch Manning: Time to Panic?▼

The “Golden Boy” of college football has been extremely underwhelming through 5 games so far this season. Now in year 3 for the Longhorns, Manning has huge expectations following a family of multiple NFL Hall of Famers. On paper, he’s got it all - big arm, great runner, sharp kid. In reality, he looks concerningly average. Surrounded by one of the most expensive rosters in all of football, Arch has no excuse to be playing at this level. Manning doesn’t even rank in the top-40 in the NCAA for Passing Yards, Touchdowns, or QBR through 5 weeks. He’s got quite a lot of work to do if he wants to live up to his uncles and grandpappy.

Colin Simmons: Not Human▲

On the other side of the ball for Texas, they’ve got something really special. Disregarding his viral “simulating using the restroom” unsportsmanlike conduct penalty against Tennessee, Simmons could be a household name on Sundays in a few years. Funny enough, the penalty may have been warranted considering he finished with 5.5 sacks. He’s got 9.5 sacks and 2 forced fumbles through five games this season and has been a nightmare for offensive tackles. I wouldn’t be surprised if Simmons ends up going top-5. He’s really that good.

Drew Mestemaker: Touchdown-Maker▲

Nobody really expected the North Texas transfer to be getting this much attention so early in the season… but this dude can ball. He can sling it, has great running ability, and looks really confident so far. I heard someone say “Phillip Rivers with wheels”. The next few weeks will be crucial to monitor Mestemaker and the Cowboys’ progress and see if the hot hand continues. Mestemaker followed both his head coach and QB coach from North Texas to Oklahoma State this off-season and their chemistry has been quite apparent so far this season.

Dylan Stewart: Hot Head with Unlimited Upside▼

What I said earlier about Colin Simmons’ potential also applies to Dylan Stewart. Stewart stands at 6’5” and 250 lbs and is the ideal prototype NFL teams are looking for in edge-rushers. However, we watched Stewart potentially tank his draft stock when the Gamecocks played Alabama earlier this season. After throwing a right hook at an Alabama player, South Carolina coaches attempted to sub out Stewart, but he wouldn’t leave the field. Once the game came to a stopping point, Stewart was greeted with a warm gesture on the sideline from his coach Shane Beamer. Stewart was given a 1-game suspension. Last weekend, Stewart followed up his suspension with an upset win against Florida where he contributed 5 tackles, 2 forced fumbles, and a sack.

Jeremiah Smith: HIM★

Just when you thought this guy couldn’t exceed your expectations anymore, he is establishing himself as one of the greatest wide receivers in the history of college football. Currently the Heisman trophy front-runner, Smith is looking to be just the third WR in the last 30 years to win the award. He is the rare type of player who can change the direction of a franchise the second he gets drafted. As an NFL GM with the 1st overall pick in the 2027 Draft, I’m not concerned with what the team’s “crucial needs” are. I’m drafting Jeremiah Smith. Get this guy even a semi-serviceable QB and he will be generational.
