On the Hill

Get Your Popcorn Ready

By Henry Houghton/Week 9

Last night we were presented with the first College Football Playoff ranking of the season! As an Alabama fan, I was thrilled with the committee’s SEC bias and my team’s placement. As for the rest of the country… I’m not so sure. The rankings are all over the place in comparison to the AP poll causing a lot of confusion. Thankfully, with so many ranked matchups this weekend, this initial ranking can be taken with a grain of salt. There is bound to be even more drama on Saturday with SIX ranked matchups between top-20 teams. Once we can get a couple more in-conference games under our belts, we’ll have a much better idea as far as what the field is going to look like. For college football fans, we are entering primetime. On Saturday, cancel your plans, stock the fridge, and get ready to stare at your TV for 12 straight hours!

Get Your Popcorn Ready

Alabama was off this weekend after a brutal stretch of games through October, and I figured it was only right that I took a bye week of my own. Crimson Tide faithful are still in recovery mode following a 20-17 loss at home to the Georgia Bulldogs and a 26-10 beatdown at Tennessee just a week later. I won’t lie, it was quite nice not having to stress over my favorite squad this week. I took care of some cleaning, played some RuneScape: Dragonwilds with the fellas, and evaluated some teams I haven’t watched this season. With that being said, Saturday cannot get here soon enough! In what is scheduled to be the final mandatory annual matchup between Alabama and LSU, the stakes couldn’t be higher. Both teams were featured in the preseason top-15 poll and despite a lot of roster questions heading into the season, both SEC powerhouses are legit championship contenders. Since then, both squads have faced two hard losses. Sadly, the playoff can’t be exclusive to the SEC and Big Ten so one of these teams will most likely miss the playoff with a loss this weekend. Obviously, there is no guarantee the victor will breeze through the rest of their season with easy wins, but I get the feeling that we’ll look back in a month at this game as a major turning point for both teams.

LSU Tigers

In the famous words of coach Kiffin: “get your popcorn ready”. The Tigers come into the weekend sitting at 6-2 and ranked #15 nationally. Nothing in Baton Rouge has generated more buzz over the past year than the arrival of Lane Kiffin. A matchup made in heaven! A greasy coach at a greasy program. Saltiness aside, Kiffin has proved to be one of the top offensive minds in all of football and recently resurrected the Ole Miss football program. Before heading to Oxford, Kiffin did a phenomenal job as the offensive coordinator at Alabama where Nick Saban resurrected his career. In his recent move to LSU, Kiffin received a lot of hate for how he handled his Ole Miss departure. As poorly as he handled the situation, Lane doesn’t owe Oxford anything; he completely brought them back on the map and they should be thanking him.

The biggest selling point this off-season in getting Kiffin to sign as head coach of the Tigers was LSU’s spending power. Louisiana State has an estimated value of $1.2 billion, more than double Ole Miss’ worth. In today’s collegiate environment, spending power is the name of the game. Kiffin wasted no time in the transfer portal upon his arrival at LSU and quickly acquired the highly sought-after transfer QB, Sam Leavitt. Leavitt had a bit of a shaky start this season, but has responded strongly in the past few games. Kiffin and Leavitt run a high-powered offense averaging 40 points per game this season. They lead the SEC at 510 yards per game and have kept defenses on their heels all season. WR Jackson Harris has had the hot hand for the Tigers with 15 catches for 263 yards and 4 TDs in the past 2 games! Watch out for Harris against a banged up Alabama secondary.

The game will be played in Baton Rouge at Death Valley with a surprising 3:30 ET kickoff. Kiffin expressed to reporters this week that he thought Alabama had the advantage with the decision to make it a daytime kickoff instead of the Tigers hosting a night game. To be fair, he has a point. That stadium is intimidating at night, especially when you’re Alabama. Give the fans a chance to spend the day getting liquored up and scream their vocal cords out at the ballgame. Maybe it will end up being a blessing for LSU to switch up the vibe, they’ve lost at home to Alabama in night games all but once in the past 15 years.

Alabama Crimson Tide

It’s been a season of a lot of growth, but also a lot of growing pains. Redshirt freshman QB Keelon Russell has been the center of the show for Alabama, showing flashes of greatness in every game. He looks like a mix of the arm of Mac Jones and the legs of Jalen Milroe, a pretty elite package. While there has been brilliance, Russell has been prone to turnovers early on. Forcing throws in pressure situations and ball security in the pocket have been an issue thus far for Russell and the Tide offense. When Alabama visited Neyland Stadium a few weeks ago, the crowd noise absolutely shut the offense down. We can only hope that Russell and OC Ryan Grubb have learned from this experience and figured out a communication plan. Death Valley, no matter the time of day, is deafening. I think this is the biggest thing to watch for Alabama this Saturday.

Star WR Ryan Coleman-Williams looks to return to the field this weekend after missing the last two games with a leg injury. He underwent a minor knee procedure but looks to be healthy and ready to go on Saturday. While Alabama is loaded at the wide receiver position and guys did a great job stepping up in Coleman-Williams’ absence, the offense has clearly missed his big-play ability. Establishing a run-game and long possessions will be the key to Alabama’s success this weekend. Keeping the LSU offense off the field needs to be the #1 priority. I think in all of the changes in recent years within the program, my favorite part has been the rise of Defensive Coordinator Kane Wommack. He has brought a new fire and discipline to the Crimson Tide defense that hasn’t been present since Saban. The ultimate goal NEEDS to be joyless murder-ball. If the secondary can stay healthy, I like Alabama’s chances of stirring things up against Leavitt and the Tigers.

Alabama Head Coach Kalen DeBoer has as much to play for this weekend as anybody. It’s crazy to think that back-to-back seasons of 9-3 isn’t getting the job done, but in Tuscaloosa, that’s not living up to the standard. I like Kalen and think he’s done about all he can stepping into a nearly impossible role to fill. However, this Saturday marks arguably the most important game of his career at Alabama and it’ll be interesting to see if he’s got his guys ready. These are the games you come to Alabama to play. You gotta want it more than the man across from you. DeBoer has been around football long enough to know what’s at stake. It’s eat or starve in Baton Rouge.

This rivalry means more to me than any game in any sport. I feel honored to have witnessed some of the battles between these two schools through the years. The TJ Yeldon ‘Grave Digger’. Zach Mettenberger crawling off the field. Ruining Leonard Fournette’s Heisman campaign. Burrow Super Team vs. Tua Super Team. It just doesn’t get better. As much as I can’t stand LSU and despise playing them, I’m going to miss playing them every year. It’s one of those traditions that is perfect the way it is. Would you separate peanut butter and jelly? Hell no. If it ain’t broke, don’t fix it. Either way, the rivalry will live on in college football history and will make their less-frequent matchups mean even more. Here’s to a great game this weekend and cheers to an incredible rivalry. Roll Tide!
