The Nirv Column

By Noah Irving/2026 Week 4

Hello familiar faces and new friends, and welcome back to the Nirv Column. Apologies to all the Nirverts out there who were waiting on something last week; it was my beautiful girlfriend’s birthday so we had our plates full while celebrating. But I’m happy to report we are back for another week of your favorite football breakdown. Shoutout Jessica, happy birthday babe!

Now I’ve been thinking, and it’s about time we talk some damn ball in this column. I’m not talking about rookies; we’ll leave that to the professionals over at our Rookie Radar. And I’m certainly not talking about any power rankings or game breakdowns; we’ll let Mr. Long handle those. I’m talking more something along the lines of mascots and nicknames.

I’m talking jerseys.

Before we get into it, just a special shoutout to the crimson and gray over on the Palouse. Absolutely putrid product they have the audacity to call a ‘football team’. Matt Miller, you’re lucky this is a family program. Blow the whole thing up and let’s lean into being a women’s soccer school.

Anyways

As the (once) great Deion Sanders said, look good play good. And I honestly believe that, that a jersey or uniform can absolutely play a role in a team’s product on the field or court. And that goes both ways: I’m a Seahawks guy through and through, but you knew that when they trotted us out in those highlighter, neon green uniforms for a Thursday night football game, you were in for it. But I want to focus on the good stuff, the big steppers, the swag surfers, the threads so damn nice you can’t blink twice. Follow me through this wonderful rabbit hole of my personal favorite sports jerseys throughout the years.

BEFORE WE REALLY START: I have to give a shoutout to the jerseys that saw the screen, but didn’t really see that play. Space Jam isn’t just my favorite all-time movie, it also produced some of the sweetest ‘fake’ basketball jerseys these eyes have seen. You’re gonna tell me a #1 Bugs jersey wouldn’t go quadruple platinum today? Or a ! Taz jersey?? Don’t even get me started on a Lola Bunny game worn….

And none of the new LeBron Space Jam crap. The ’96, original, MJ vs the MonStars stuff. Straight into my veins.

And of course, these honorable mentions wouldn’t be complete without the likes of the metallic yellow Average Joe’s pinnies, the Flint Tropics threads, and we gotta give it up for the blue Sunnyvale hockey sweaters (shoutout Ricky). The Leafs need to figure out a way to incorporate that; or I guess make a run in the playoffs. That might be too far-fetched, though.

Now, let’s get into some actual jerseys that made it to the field.

We’ll start with an easy one: the OG Raptors jersey. Purple, with the #15 Carter on the back.

Now I may be a bit biased because I fucking love dinosaurs, but we’ve got a raptor damn near high stepping his way through a fastbreak to start us off. Couple that with the deep purple jersey and bright red accents; there are some things so perfect that don’t need to be altered, and it’s a shame the folks up in Toronto felt like they needed a rebrand on something already this perfect. Sidebar for the people: they just retired Kyle Lowry’s number, and honored him with a statue of himself…..as a raptor. Say what you want about Canada, but they know how to treat their stars.

In a similar vein, quick shoutout to these other incredible NBA jerseys that hopefully get to see the light of day again:

Jazz purple mountains (please disregard one of the two men in this photo)

Nuggets rainbow skyline

Just about any Sonics jersey; though I do like the red accent more than the all greens

Switching gears over to football, let’s start with the NFL:

Hate to say it, but the Patriots red jerseys are very clean. Anytime you can get Pat the Patriot on the helmet, you simply have to

And this list would be null and void if we didn’t include the powder blues….

….But are those even the best powder blues…?

And NO, I did not forget about the creamsicles. These alone are worth at least another 5 points

Sticking with football, the college world is no stranger to some insane uniform combos. Even the attention to detail with just some helmets, you really can see some beautiful art on that turf from a team that is 1-9 but just happy to be dressed out. Shoutout the fine people at Western Kentucky who seem to have leaned into Big Red quite a bit lately. No complaints here.

And as dogshit as they may be, anytime the Cougs don the cursive script, I’ll be feeling something. Bonus points for the rose

And honestly, this college football list could include only Oregon uniforms. I’m not saying they hit every time, but just based on the sheer volume of combos they are pumping out, they have definitely had some bangers. Like when they dressed up as a duck

Or when they dressed up like Baylor but slapped UO on their helmets

Or when they dressed up like WVU but called it the Webfoots

A recent one, but gotta give it up to the Green Wave for their Mardi Gras threads

I’m also a sucker for some pinstripes, and I do not think it gets any better than the old Diamondbacks ones

And of course, have to give the Pirates a shout strictly for the hat alone
