Dan Quinn Must Be Banished to the Shadow Realm

By Adam Long/Week 5

So, fair warning here with this one. If you’re coming to this article expecting objective coverage or unbiased analytics pertaining to the Week 5 matchup between the Washington Commanders and the New York Giants, today, that is absolutely not what you’re going to get. I am a diehard Washington football fan, and even through the trials and tribulations of the last two decades, I don’t think I’ve ever been more disgusted by this team than I was with their loss today. Yes. I know it was a tie. But don’t get it twisted, this was a LOSS. A big fat L. We had every single opportunity to beat an inferior opponent today and squandered every single one of them. It’s not going to go on the record as a loss, and yes, the NFC East is the most hilariously hapless division in football so the season is not officially over. But this is as pathetic of an ending to a professional football game that you’re ever going to see watching this game.

Now, I don’t want to go too hard on any player or players in particular here, because I thought individually, we had some pretty stellar performances today. The highlight was obviously quarterback Jayden Daniels, who had one of his most dynamic games of his young career. He was effective throwing the football and moving the chains and scored on two electric long touchdown runs. The defense was all-world for the first three quarters, and I specifically want to call out cornerback Amik Robertson, who through five weeks is one of the most impactful coverage guys in the NFL. However, the Commanders snatched defeat from the jaws of victory today due to the complete and utter ineptitude of this kindergarten coaching staff.

First off, Daronte Jones. What in the name of God are you doing out there? You called a great game defensively until it mattered. 10 total points allowed through 52 minutes. That’s excellent. Then you called soft zone coverages, got too conservative with your blitz packages, and allowed JAMEIS WINSTON to go down the field and dice your defense apart three straight times for touchdowns to allow the Giants to rally back from down 27-13 with 8 minutes remaining. How the hell do you leave Isaiah Likely alone on seemingly every single play? He had a CAREER high in catches today. Also, Daronte, here’s a fun stat for you. Mikey Sainristil is 5’9”. Malik Nabers is 6’0”. I don’t know if you’re a big math guy, but that’s a difference of three inches. That’s a lot! Maybe don’t line up our most beleaguered corner on one of the most dynamic young receivers in the game in a third and long (nice) situation in the red area. We looked completely lost out there defensively when it mattered, and that falls to you.

Daronte, you’re guilty, but you’re not the only one. David Blough, are you listening? Let me tell you something about football, and offensive playcalling in general. When a defense is blitzing you (a blitz is where a defense sends more than three or four guys on a pass rush, in case you didn’t know), you can still be effective in your air attack by calling quick passes. Think stuff that develops quickly, like screens or slants. What you don’t want to do is call long developing play-action deep passes when the Giants are sending seven to eight guys on every passing down. What you REALLY don’t want to do is continue to call those plays on five consecutive drives when you need to score points, even after those plays obviously continue to not work.

OK, OK, so those are just the coordinators. What about the big boss? The head honcho? Mr. 28-3 himself? Dan Quinn, you must be fired immediately. Not tomorrow morning, not at the bye, not in the offseason. Tonight. Don’t even let him leave the stadium with his job. Now we played poorly in the fourth quarter today, and we had a lot of opportunities to win and lose this game until the last fifty seconds. I have never seen a more idiotic sequence of events than what occurred at the end of this game, and the blame falls squarely with Mr. Quinn in that regard. You are the head coach. The buck stops with you. Yes, Jayden and the rest of the offense were sorely lacking situational awareness in the final minute. But we had the ball right outside the red area with a minute left to go, and we didn’t win the football game. We didn’t miss a kick to tie. We didn’t even TRY. When you have the ball in a sudden death situation like that and the kick becomes a chip shot, YOU KICK THE FUCKING FIELD GOAL. You got too cute with it, and didn’t communicate Pop Warner-level clock-management stuff to your players and assistants.

Today is October 11, 2026. Dan Quinn was fired by the Atlanta Falcons on October 11, 2020. Do you remember any other poor clock management situations from his tenure in the big A? I do. Quinn has been saddled with a football curse since the comeback in the Super Bowl, and we will never achieve real success with him leading this team. I’m sorry Dan, you really do seem like a nice guy. And 2024 was awesome, but I give most of the credit to Jayden on that one. I cannot and will not put up with this week after week, we have been an absolute dumpster fire since the NFC Championship game, and you and the front office have seemingly done nothing to ameliorate the multiple problems on this roster.

Daronte and David, I recognize that you two are in your first year calling plays, so I’ll let you off the hook this time and give you time to develop. There are plenty of things I’m seeing from both units that were really encouraging. I’m just angry. You know, hurt people hurt people, and all that.

Josh Harris. Make the call. Bring Sean McDermott in, and banish Quinn to the shadow realm.
