The Nirv Column

Around the League

By Noah Irving/2026 Week 5

First and foremost, if the Washington State Cougars decide to trot out Pinnick again at ANY point this year, we’ll bring J6 to Pullman directly into Kirby Moore’s office.

Now that that is out of the way, welcome to yet another rendition of the Nirv Column. We decided to take a bit of a different approach this week, and actually talk some ball. Fret not, I’m not the one doing the talking. Instead, we took to the streets, to see what other NFL fans around the league are feeling about their team. Editor’s note: these questions were asked prior to the week 5 games playing out. I’m sure we would have some updates if we followed up after Sunday’s batch of crap.

I wanted to get a quick pulse check after a few games under our belts. Below are some answers from different fans around the league, to the questions everyone has been wondering:

Do you like where your team is at right now? What changes, if any, would you make?

What is the best red food?

(Second editor’s note: in case you haven’t heard already, they found a new species of penguin. Check ’em out here.)

Now, to hear what you all have to say on the state of your teams:

Huber K (Chicago Bears)

I love that the Bears are still able to win games with where they are at right now. Of course I would love to see Caleb Williams out there but the offense appears efficient under Ben Johnson even without him. Our defense has shown signs of being much better than last year. We led the league in takeaways but the points allowed were killing us. Which after the Panthers game I thought was still going to be an issue. I’d say if I were to rate it out of 10, I’d probably go 6.5/10 given that I want Caleb out there and I’ll probably never forgive Sam Roush.

Best red food: Strawberries are definitely up there for me but if I could pick anything probably Chamorro Empanadas, they’re pretty red/orange in comparison to traditional empanadas.

Spencer T (Las Vegas Raiders)

How do I like where my team is at. Happy? Yes. Furious? Also yes. Being 3-1 as a Raiders fan is weird because every win still feels like a hostage situation. Back-to-back special teams turnovers is the football equivalent of setting your own kitchen on fire then being shocked there’s smoke in the house. We literally gift-wrapped points for the Chiefs. You can’t spot Mahomes opportunities and expect him to politely decline. That said, Kirko Chainz has me believing again. Klint Kubiak’s offense actually looks like it was designed by people who have watched football before. For the first time in a long time, the Raiders look competent, which honestly might be the scariest part. So yeah, I’m loving where we’re at. If we could stop treating special teams like a weekly charity event, I’d be even happier.

Spencer then went on to describe his intramural softball team:

For my softball league teams, I’m somehow both happy and annoyed. My Tuesday men’s team (Pitches be Crazy) finished 3rd, which sounds great until you realize we absolutely should’ve finished 1st. A couple of crappy innings turned us ahead 4 runs to losing by 2. It’s like getting a bronze medal after tripping over your own shoelaces on the way to gold. It’s sad because it wasn’t even pitches that were crazy, it was poor fielding in routine plays. My Wednesday co-ed team (Battered Souls) went undefeated all season, so naturally our reward was…not getting to play in the playoffs because of scheduling conflicts. Apparently our toughest opponent this year wasn’t another team, it was a damn calendar. If I could change anything? I’d like a scheduling coordinator who understands that adults have jobs, families, and about 17 other commitments beyond chasing a yellow ball around a diamond. Overall though, one team finished 3rd when they should have been 1st, and the other never lost a game. I’ll take it. I just wish our season didn’t end because we got defeated by logistics instead of another team.

Best red food: I’ve always said Italian is something I think I could eat every day for the rest of my life but as I’ve gotten older the red sauces have started haunting me and popping Pepcid and Tums like it’s Halloween night. The indigestion is too much so I prob lean towards steak or burgers although it’s technically more pink than red I like medium rare so some red there and then paired with a nice Cab. I did make Brook a nice stuffed bell pepper chicken parm that was very delicious but haunted me later.

Justin B (Denver Broncos)

Thanks for the opportunity to answer such an important question.

Looking at the state of the Denver Broncos so far this season I would say I’m underwhelmed. The defense has been good but not top 3 as expected. Granted they have played a very difficult schedule to open the year. A lot rides on Pat Surtain getting healthy.

The offense has basically been a middle school offense. No down field throws. So safe. So conservative. No points. Sean needs to take back the play calling. Bo showed last year he can make things happen and put up points if you let him go for it. Sure there will be mistakes but he also has more game winning drives than almost everyone in the league over the last 5 years or so. I’d rather lose taking some shots than lose running RB flat and 8 yard crossing routes.

Not a supey year like I hoped sadly. Not sure where they go from here to crack into that level.

Best red food: Alaskan King crab. Gail said Twizzlers.

Michael C (Chicago Bears)

Absolutely. I was excited for the offense and dreading watching the defense going into the year. The offense has surpassed or at the very least met high expectations, which includes games with 2 different backup quarterbacks. And then the defense has been playing much better than I expected. Week 1 was a rough go, but since, they’ve been an average to above average defense while limited talent wise. I still want them to add a big name and some depth in the front 7, but I cannot complain about how they have looked. If the offense keeps this pace, an average defense will be enough for the Bears to be at least interesting in the playoffs. I had never experienced Bears coaches that can elevate both sides of the ball to this level but Ben Johnson and Dennis Allen have been unbelievable. Not necessarily saying they’re a top Super Bowl contender this year but I’m very excited for the next few years with this team.

Best red food: Lasagna.

Cole F (Washington State Cougars)

First on the agenda, exchange Matt Miller’s headset for a vacuum, he would be more of a benefit to the team cleaning the locker room rather than calling plays. Second, seems like Pinnick might have some dirt on Kirby Moore (only reason I see why he hasn’t been benched) so pull a little Tonya Harding and get him out of commission. Third, see how offense looks against Utah State but if it looks the same as before complete overhaul and bust out the good ol triple option with Duggar. Absolutely hate where we’re at right now.

Best red food: Hmm, maybe a good bolognese with pasta if that’s pushing the red agenda, salsa is another one that comes to mind.

Paul E (LA Chargers)

No I do not. The game has passed Jim by. The 4th down decision making is archaic, the press conferences are a mess. He genuinely looks like he has no idea what’s going on up there. The hallmark of a Jim Harbaugh football team is supposed to be toughness and mistake free football. The Chargers are a disaster operationally, the interior of the O-Line has been neglected for years, and he hired an offensive coordinator that was supposed to elevate a top 5 talent at quarterback, and instead he changed his footwork and tried to turn him into a timing anticipation thrower. Joe Hortiz is cocky in press conferences when he hasn’t hit on a draft pick not named Joe Alt in three years. Tear the entire thing down and sell the team while they’re at it.

Best red food: This is New Mexico as fuck, but carne adovada with red chile.

Adam L (Washington Commanders)

I could probably answer this question with nuance, I could probably answer this question with a million different things that have gone right and gone wrong over the last three seasons (and I will), but to start off I will answer this question with a fairly firm and resounding No. Although, to be fair, as a lifelong fan of the Washington Redskins/Football Team/Commanders, I have never really ever been able to answer Yes to this specific question. I can remember two times specifically in which I remember being able to answer in the affirmative, once during the 2012 season, when we were riding high off the dazzling rookie season of RGIII, and once in 2024, when we rode a different rookie QB all the way to the NFC championship in a run nobody outside of Ashburn could’ve possibly predicted.

After that beautiful run in Jayden Daniels’ rookie year, where we defied all odds and he brought us from a 4-13 2023 campaign to a 14-6 season and one game from our first Super Bowl appearance since 1992, things have been tenuous in the nation’s capital. Our front office spent their first offseason post-NFC championship building the wrong way, where GM Adam Peters put a premium on signing free agents from yesterday in order to build around the now second-year signal caller. See Deebo Samuel, Von Miller, and Marshon Lattimore. These signings weren’t disastrous, as Miller was our sack leader last year (I know, ouch) and Samuel was largely serviceable when he was available. We won’t talk about Lattimore. The most notable problem with this team has been building through the draft (an issue inherited from previous head coach Ron Rivera, who did not hit on a single first-round draft pick during his four-year tenure), although the last two classes look much better than in years past. Last year saw the additions of a starting tackle (Josh Conerly), a starting corner (Trey Amos, will be elite when he returns from injury, had a great rookie year), and a starting running back (Bill Croskey-Merritt). This draft was fine, but 2026 looks to be even better with the most notable addition of LB Sonny Styles from Ohio State, who we selected with the seventh overall pick. He is probably the brightest spot on this team as of Week 4 of the 2026 season.

So with these relatively successful draft classes recently, you might wonder why my outlook seems so sour, especially for a team that has so recently gone deep into the postseason. This roster is not good. Injuries are rampant, and significant injuries to a team whose roster isn’t deep enough in the first place are a death sentence to any team, let alone a squad that won five games in 2025. Outside of Daniels, McLaurin, Tunsil, Cosmi, and Amos, the lack of star power leaves too much to be desired, especially considering that all five of those previously mentioned players, who are probably the five best players on the team, are currently injured. Daniels, who is the primary superstar, is the one that makes fans the most nervous, as he missed 10 games last year in some capacity and will probably miss his third game of the year on Sunday already. JD5 is an incredible football player, but anyone who knows the NFL knows that the best ability is availability, and if your best player(s) can’t get on the field week in and week out, then you don’t really have a competitive pro football team.

I really hope that these guys can get back on the field soon, especially with how weak the division looks this year, but starting the year off once again bitten by the injury bug and holding a 1-3 record does not inspire much confidence. Hopefully I can eat my words when Jayden comes back in Week 5 against the Giants, goes for 6 total touchdowns en route to an MVP season. What’s being a fan if you’re not smoking at least a little bit of hopium?

Best red food: pizza. Or any big red bug.

And clean up penalties to keep the defense fresh. And I’d bring back the white throwbacks like yesterday.

Our program in particular I think needs a revamp, seems to be getting a tad stale so looking into redesigning the playbook and practice structure for next year.

Best red food eh? Basic bitch but I go crazy for lasagna.

Alex H (Seattle Seahawks)

I sure liked how the Seahawks looked week 1&2 more than 3&4. We can easily chalk that up to RB & safety injuries, and Sam Darnold being shaky sure. But what I need to see this next week against SF offensively is solidifying our WR2. JSN is on a generational run, but I am really looking for Horton or Shaheed to take over this divisional showdown. We have been getting KILLED on 3rd and long all year, I need to see a WR2 step up and make some plays. If the run game isn’t going to be there this year, we need to make up for it with all the talent we have in WR depth, because what we’ve seen so far ain’t cutting it.

Best red food: Strawberries. Including that one strawberry candy. Also strawberry pop tart. Mmmmm.

Reece R (video games)

Not an easy task but I think we have a winner for favorite game and only one super small change. I think we have to break it down into a few categories a little bit though.

  • Story
  • Gameplay (how fun is it to just run and jump around)
  • Replay-ability
  • Nostalgia
  • Hours of fun

Hands down the winner for me has to be Halo Reach. I thought back on a lot of fun I’ve had playing games over the years but something about Halo Reach always stuck with me. The way Bungie was in the flow state by the 4th halo game is something I feel like we might never see again in our lifetime.

Halo 1 changed shooter games forever, Halo 2 was the grandfather of Xbox Live multiplayer gaming (plus dual SMGs) then they drop Halo 3 on us with the best multiplayer and create Forge mode. So Halo Reach has to now follow up possibly the greatest trilogy in gaming with one of the most iconic main characters ever.

Luckily Bungie delivered with the final Halo game they ever made and gave us a masterpiece. Something about that reveal that a team of the coolest 6 Spartans are the only reason humanity even had a chance with good ole Master Chief. I can still remember how shocked I was after watching each of the Noble team deaths (besides Jun, I’m pretty sure he got away after Halsey gives Jorge Cortana) Jorge throwing you back to earth out the side of Covenant ship that blows up only to reveal 1000 more. Or when Kat gets sniped out of nowhere mid sentence. Commander went out like a badass just to give you a few extra seconds and then the boy Emile taking out ships for you until the last possible minute. Then that last mission Lone Wolf where you realize as a kid there is no winning this game and the win was getting Cortana off the planet, and then they just hit you with that helmet sitting in the mud with the mountain in the background, absolutely masterpiece.

That’s just for story and then we get into why this game really wins and that’s because it sweeps the last 4 categories. Sure Minecraft is fun but Halo Reach’s forge mode gave me and boys hundreds of hours of fun and that same feeling you get in Minecraft.

Then you get the buttery gameplay and the addition of the armor upgrades. Nothing like sending your decoy out in swat just to see someone walk right in front of you for the cleanest headshot ever. Still to this day no multiplayer game competes with the fun I had getting a 20 piece in Halo Reach swat. It still has insane replay-ability too. Every time I go back I can get into a lobby or even just run a campaign mission for a shot of nostalgia.

I think hours of fun and nostalgia go hand in hand too. There was something incredible about the way me and the boys could lock into a forge build for hours on end. The amount of mazes and obstacle courses we made and tested for what seemed like weeks on end was some of the best times I’ve ever had gaming. Then there’s going on Live with a headset and talking crazy shit playing 4 player split screen. Or every time we would play 4 player split screen and get mad because the god damn kill feed would go directly over your gun sights. And then the whole world of firefight was hours of more fun when the other stuff finally got old. The rotation in this game was crazy and it just never got old, nothing like it and don’t think there ever can be again.

Only change I would make to this masterpiece is to make leveling up easier because god damn that was a grind, otherwise 10/10 RIP Bungie made Halo games.

Best red food: Easy…Honeycrisp apple.

Shawn P (LA Rams)

Don’t love where the Rams are at but don’t hate it. They are one bad PI call away from being 3-1.

As long as Myles Garrett comes back healthy we should be in good shape for down the stretch. McDuffie is a beast. And the offense should be clicking finally now that Puka is back.

Best red food: Strawberries.
