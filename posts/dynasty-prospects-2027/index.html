Three Dynasty Prospects to Watch for 2027

By Daniel Brents/2027 Draft

It is never too early to begin thinking about the 2027 NFL Draft. A highly esteemed class of skill position players led by what we believe to be a generational prospect in Jeremiah Smith, but what kind of sports advisor would I be if I told you to make sure Jeremiah Smith is on your radar? Let me dive a little deeper and make sure these three guys are players you are aware of.

KJ Duff, WR, Rutgers

KJ Duff is not a sleeper by any means, but the 6’ 6” 225-pound wideout will not get the same national recognition as players like Smith, Coleman, or RCW. Duff is currently ranked 4th in receiving yards with an impressive slash of 33/628/7. Watch the tape; Duff rarely has to utilize his massive frame as his above-average speed and solid route running allows him to create separation with ease.

NFL Comparison: Drake London, a big-body WR that can run the full route tree.

Ideal NFL Team: Seattle Seahawks, a team in need of a WR2 who can stretch the field for JSN.

Isaac Brown, RB, Louisville

Speed kills, baby. Brown is the fastest RB in the 2027 NFL class, and his breakaway speed leads to massive chunk plays. Against Florida State, Brown went for 235 yards on 17 carries, scoring twice. Brown had a thousand-yard season with Louisville in 2024, but slightly regressed in 2025. The big RB names in this class are out of the SEC, but with Hardy likely returning to Missouri, Brown will likely shoot up the board.

NFL Comparison: De’Von Achane, one cut running back with elite speed.

Ideal NFL Team: Baltimore Ravens, a nightmare of a pace switch up between an aging Henry.

DJ Vonnahme, TE, Iowa

Who doesn’t like an Iowa TE? A good old-fashioned tight end factory from Iowa has produced another NFL prospect. DJ is listed at 6’ 4”, 240-pound which is on the smaller side of this year’s TE class. I believe he fits the mold of the modern TE, one who is built for route running and less for goal line sets. DJ is a great blocker, as are almost all Iowa tight ends, but his strong point is his versatility as a pass catcher. He can catch passes out of the backfield, on the line of scrimmage, or even in the slot.

NFL Comparison: Dalton Schultz, a useful matchup asset that can be reliable out of multiple formations.

Ideal NFL Team: Kansas City Chiefs, time to replace Kelce with a versatile vertical threat TE.
