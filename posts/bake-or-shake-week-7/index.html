Bake or Shake

Mayfield 7:1

By Nate Kjormoe/Week 7

“And on the 7th week, he rose again.”

Mayfield 7:1

I sat down last Sunday to watch good ol’ Baker Mayfield make his return to the gridiron after his three-week absence. As I attempted to find my journalistic nature, I grabbed a notebook and planned to take some extensive, detailed notes on the game. Recording every dropback, pass, completion, incompletion, etc., that Baker made that afternoon.

Then I remembered…

This is BAKE or SHAKE!

We don’t take notes here, we don’t deeply pore over the stats, and we certainly do not do simple math while watching Baker Mayfield play fucking football!

Hell, we don’t even make rational decisions when watching Baker play football.

So here we are again, zero notes and pure belief in the return of Baker Mayfield and hope for this Tampa Bay team. Now, as we dive into Baker’s Week 7 performance, let’s do a quick review of the numbers.

Baker threw for a respectable 64 passing yards… ah shit, another SHAKE?

Not on my watch.

While 64 yards might not jump off the page, there is more to this story than meets the eye. The story on October 25th, 2026, was of a team that finally learned to play as a unit. The Buccaneers’ offensive line had one of its strongest performances of the season in Week 7. Their line dominated the Panthers’ defensive front from start to finish, helping Tampa rush for 258 yards, including 171 yards before contact, and 3 rushing touchdowns!

IF IT’S NOT BROKE, DON’T FUCKING FIX IT!

With Tampa’s O-line dominating in the trenches of Verdun, and their lead back Bucky Irving rushing for 106 yards on 24 carries, why the hell does Baker need to throw the damn ball?

For Baker, it’s hard to succeed when the goalposts are always moving. Oh, how easily people forget what Baker has accomplished throughout his career. And this week, he returned in just three weeks from a severe thumb injury and did what he needed to do.

And what did he do? Glad you asked!

Baker Mayfield threw his 64 complimentary passing yards with a 109.7 passer rating! That outperformed Brock Purdy! You know, the guy some people apparently think is the second coming of Joe Montana!

Baker also threw for a touchdown, maneuvered when needed, didn’t take a single sack, and had 0 turnovers (interceptions… or fumbles! I knew you would ask).

Baker did not need to throw for 300 yards and 3 touchdowns in this game. His team had their leaders back, knew to trust in his return, and played three phases of amazing football. Hell, the defense got in on the fun with five sacks, two interceptions, and a defensive touchdown of their own!

Incredible how one man can turn around the energy of a team.

Coulda swore there was a reason they paid him 165 million dollars?

OH RIGHT!

Because he’s the heart of the fucking team, and when he’s on the field, the boys in red and orange know they can run through a wall, because they know Baker would!

And that, my friends, is exactly what the Buccaneers did. They ran through a wall, and another, and another after that, until the clock hit 0:00 and they had crumbled the Carolina Panthers to rubble. Defeating them 43-13.

That’s a 30-point victory on Baker’s return.

A quiet night for Baker, maybe, but there were fireworks my friends.

With just 12 seconds remaining in the first half, Baker, rather than taking the easy way out and throwing quickly from the pocket, scrambled to his right. With the eye of the tiger, he located Tez Johnson in the end zone for a 14-yard dagger with only 4 seconds left. Smothering the Panthers’ hopes and dreams and converting them to the truth of Baker Mayfield.

Last week I called upon Tampa’s team to show up for Baker, and for the first time this season, they did.

But what was most important is that Mr. Mayfield himself came back! He toughed out the pain, pushed aside the drama, and did what he was born to do: play football. His team came to play for him, especially his O-line, who buried that personal vendetta of theirs I mentioned last week and finally blocked for their leader.

Did I not say:

“If Tampa Bay can figure out how to protect its quarterback for more than three seconds… there’s no reason this season can’t turn around.”

Bake or Shake, Weeks 1–3

And they certainly fucking did on Sunday afternoon.

Moving forward, I expect nothing but the best from Baker. With a team that’s finally backing him up and his thumb healing, the sky isn’t even the limit for this cast-down, unsung hero.

As the great Commissioner Gordon once said… kinda…

“Baker isn’t the hero Tampa Bay deserves, but he’s the one it needs right now.”

Commissioner Gordon

I stand behind Baker Mayfield moving into Week 8 against the Falcons. And he is once again going to prove the doubters wrong and deliver us our second BAKE of the season!

Oh, did I not already mention? As if you couldn’t tell.

Week 7 Verdict: THAT’S A MF BAKEEE!

Ya boi, Nathanael Alan Kjormoe
