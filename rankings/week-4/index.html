Power Rankings

After Week 4

By Adam Long

Alrighty folks, another week of pro ball is in the books and boy do I have some updates for you! Plenty of movement all over the power ranking leaderboard, although this week provided a little less confusion in terms of basically every game being an upset. I think it’s still just a tad too early to start making concrete declarations about Who’s Hot and Who’s Not at this juncture, but the fog of the first month of the season is beginning to subside. We are a long way off from predicting playoff seeding or draft order, but I don’t think anyone would tell me I’m taking crazy pills if I told you the Lombardi Trophy is probably not making the trip to Nashville or Tampa in 2027.

Let’s get into the games. The Steelers and Browns kicked off the week on Thursday night in Believeland, a close matchup which saw Aaron Rodgers and Mike McCarthy exchanging some colorful sideline pleasantries, and Deshaun Watson continuing to play solid enough football (Solid, not good. There’s a difference!) to earn his team a victory and improve to 3-1 (?????). Indy and Washington played in what was likely the worst game of the week, an unfortunate contest for Commanders fans who saw their backup QB Marcus Mariota go down with what looks like a fairly significant knee injury. Indiana Jones and the Colts (AND THE REFS!!!… ok sorry I’m biased, shoot me) got the win thanks to Jonathan Taylor doing whatever he wanted to a Washington run defense that’s been surprisingly stout this season. Baltimore Ravens fans can breathe a sigh of relief after barely knocking off the cellar-dwelling Titans, as QB Lamar Jackson’s injury towards the end of the game is only an ankle sprain that will have him week to week. Tennessee still looks inept, surprise surprise. The Bears, led by backup QB Tyson Bagent (you sensing a theme here yet?) gave it to the J-E-T-S, and Chicago improved to 3-1 after only playing six quarters of football with Caleb Williams so far this season. A surprise in Buffalo, where Drake Maye finally clocked out from Jersey Mike’s and led a 10-play, 73-yard drive to beat the Bills in Buffalo on a game-winning touchdown strike to WR Efton Chism III. The story with the Jags and the Bengals continued the trend of Refball dominating Week 4, but the Duval faithful grabbed another win to improve to 3-1. Vibes are subpar in Cincinnati, as both superstar wide receivers for Who Dey nation are out for the foreseeable future. Insult to injury, or whatever. On to the battle of Texas, where the Cowboys beat the Texans 34-30 behind the ridiculous 14-catch, 189-yard performance of league-leading receiver CeeDee Lamb. The Texans fell to 0-4 and all but dashed their high preseason expectations. I don’t have a lot to say about the Cardinals-Giants contest, aside from being impressed by the resilience of this Giants team and being depressed by the quality of Arizona’s defense. New York got the win and improved to 3-1 (?????). The battle between Philadelphia and the LA Rams was a matchup between two teams that are probably good but you wouldn’t know it by watching their games this season. The Rams squeaked it out to improve to 2-2, still alive in the ultra-competitive NFC West. I basically have nothing to say about Packers-Buccaneers. Congrats Packers fans, you beat an undrafted rookie quarterback and a terrible football team by three points. Yikes. Bad vibes in Cheese Land.

Onto the late slate, Minnesota improved to 4-0 behind a stellar defensive performance to defeat the Dolphins. Despite their unblemished record, the Vikings’ offensive ineptitude needs to be fixed, and fast, if they want to actually be competitive in January. The Chiefs defeated the Raiders in what was my most-excited-for game this week, where I was very interested to see how this previously undefeated Vegas club would fare against a competent team. Although they didn’t get the win, I’m very impressed and encouraged by what I saw from the Raiders, who very nearly completed a fourth-quarter comeback in a game that could’ve gone either way. Next, we make for the Pacific Northwest, where the defending champs bounced back with a win against the hapless Chargers. Although the scoreboard made it look closer than it was for most of this one, Seattle cruised to a one-score victory behind the strength of their defense. Staying on the West Coast, the 49ers beat the Broncos to improve to 4-0 in a game that was firmly controlled by San Francisco for all 60 minutes. Brock Purdy should be everyone’s MVP frontrunner at this point in the season, dude is playing essentially perfect football. The Panthers beat the Lions in Charlotte, and you can hear all about that in my Game of the Week column.

Finally, Week 4 was capped off in NOLA, where the Saints celebrated the 20th anniversary of the Superdome reopening after Hurricane Katrina by getting thoroughly spanked by the rival Atlanta Falcons. Bijan Robinson cemented himself as the best running back in the league, call it the eye test, but there really isn’t anyone else in football moving like him with the ball in his hands. So there you have it, a rapid fire recap of all the games from this week. Maybe next week I’ll try and throw some more name puns in there for all you Fastest Two Minutes fans. Maybe I won’t. I’m not your dad.
